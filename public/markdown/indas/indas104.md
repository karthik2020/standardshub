# Insurance Contracts

Governs accounting for insurance contracts, converged with IFRS 4 (not yet with IFRS 17), with distinctive application limited by IRDAI's separate regulatory framework for Indian insurers.

## Notice

**warning:** Important Indian modifications – a notable time-lag from global IFRS

Ind AS 104 remains converged with the SUPERSEDED IFRS 4, not the current IFRS 17. India has NOT yet notified an Ind AS 117 equivalent, and the MCA has repeatedly deferred full Ind AS applicability to insurance companies. IRDAI's own regulatory framework governs licensed Indian insurers in practice.
