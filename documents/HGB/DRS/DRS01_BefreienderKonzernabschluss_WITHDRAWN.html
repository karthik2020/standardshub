DRS 1 / DRS 1a — Befreiender Konzernabschluss nach § 292a HGB
Exempting consolidated financial statements under §292a HGB (+ companion standard: Goodwill and other intangible assets)
⛔ WITHDRAWN — via DRÄS 2
Part of the DRS consolidation wave
DRS 1 and its companion DRS 1a were among the DRSC's earliest standards (1998–2000 era), addressing a narrow and now-obsolete transitional question: German parent companies that prepared their Konzernabschluss under an internationally recognised framework (rather than HGB's own §§290 et seq.) could, under the old §292a HGB, be EXEMPTED from the domestic German consolidation requirements entirely. DRS 1/1a supplied the detailed conditions for this exemption, with DRS 1a specifically addressing how goodwill and other intangible assets should be treated within that exempting framework.
Why withdrawn
§292a HGB was itself a TRANSITIONAL provision, bridging the period before EU Regulation 1606/2002 made IFRS mandatory for listed groups' consolidated accounts (the "dual system" this whole HGB poster series has documented via §315e). Once §315e's own, more comprehensive IFRS-permission framework was firmly established and §292a itself was repealed, DRS 1 and DRS 1a lost their entire legal anchor and were formally withdrawn via DRÄS 2 (a Deutscher Rechnungslegungs Änderungsstandard — an "amendment standard," the DRSC's own mechanism for updating or retiring existing DRS, directly analogous to how IASB uses annual improvements or amendment standards to update IFRS).
