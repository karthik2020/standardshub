# Consolidated Financial Statements

Establishes principles for presenting and preparing consolidated financial statements when a public-sector entity controls one or more other entities – including the three-element control test, non-controlling interests, changes in ownership, loss of control, the investment-entity exception, and a public-sector 'combinations carve-out'. Effective 1 January 2017. (IFRS 10 equivalent.) Disclosure of interests in other entities is deferred to IPSAS 38.

IPSAS 35 Consolidated Financial Statements establishes the principles that a public-sector entity applies in presenting and preparing its consolidated financial statements when it controls one or more other entities. It defines control, sets out the three-element control test (power, returns and the ability to use power), requires consolidation of controlled entities, addresses non-controlling interests and changes in ownership, the derecognition and gain/loss mechanics on loss of control, the investment-entity fair-value exception, transitional provisions, and a public-sector carve-out for certain combinations. Disclosure requirements for interests in other entities are deferred to IPSAS 38.
