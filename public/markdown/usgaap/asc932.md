# ASC 932 – Extractive Activities–Oil and Gas

Establishes the dual successful efforts and full cost methods for oil and gas exploration and production accounting.

ASC 932 permits oil and gas entities to choose between the successful efforts and full cost methods for capitalizing exploration costs. It establishes the ceiling test under full cost and contrasts with IFRS 6, noting the strong prevalence of successful efforts in the US versus different practices under IFRS.

## Notice

**warning:** Divergence from IFRS 6/IAS 36

ASC 932 allows a dual method choice (successful efforts or full cost) while IFRS 6 generally leads to different capitalization practices. The ceiling test under full cost also diverges from IAS 36's impairment model.
