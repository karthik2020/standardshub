# Introduction to Recommended Practice Guidelines

Sets out the purpose, mandate, and non-authoritative good-practice status of Recommended Practice Guidelines (RPGs) issued by the IPSASB for General Purpose Financial Reports (GPFRs) that are not financial statements.

The Introduction to Recommended Practice Guidelines provides the overarching context for the RPG series issued by the IPSASB. It explains the Board's public interest objective, establishes the critical distinction between authoritative IPSAS (which govern general purpose financial statements) and non-authoritative RPGs (which provide good-practice guidance for broader GPFRs without establishing binding requirements), and introduces RPGs 1 through 3.
