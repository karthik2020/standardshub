# FRS 103 – Insurance Contracts | Extended Reference

FRS 103 supplements FRS 102 or FRS 101 for entities issuing insurance contracts, largely permitting continuation of existing accounting practices.

FRS 103 applies to entities that issue insurance or reinsurance contracts, supplementing whichever primary standard otherwise applies. It largely permits insurers to continue their existing accounting policies for insurance liabilities, subject to a minimum liability adequacy test each reporting date, and is based on the superseded IFRS 4 model rather than the current IFRS 17 architecture. This represents a significant practical divergence for UK insurers reporting under FRS 102/103 compared to their group parents or international peers using IFRS 17.

## Notice

**warning:** FRS 103 vs IFRS 17

FRS 103 is based on the superseded IFRS 4 model and has not adopted IFRS 17's comprehensive new insurance measurement architecture. This creates a practical divergence for UK insurers, who continue to apply a continuation-of-practice approach unlike the fulfillment cash flow plus contractual service margin model required under full IFRS.
