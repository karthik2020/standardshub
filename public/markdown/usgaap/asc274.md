# Personal Financial Statements

Governs the preparation and presentation of personal financial statements for individuals, including the statement of financial condition and estimated current value measurement.

ASC 274 governs the preparation and presentation of personal financial statements for individuals or related groups, focusing on the statement of financial condition measured at estimated current value.
