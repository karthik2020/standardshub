# Segment Reporting

Prescribes disclosure of information about the differing parts (segments) of an entity, including identification of segments, their profit/loss and assets, and internal allocation bases. Effective 1 July 2003.

IPSAS 18 Segment Reporting prescribes disclosures that enable users to evaluate the nature and financial effects of the different parts of an entity's operations and the segments' contributions.
