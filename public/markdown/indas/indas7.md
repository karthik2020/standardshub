# Statement of Cash Flows

Requires presentation of cash flows classified into operating, investing and financing activities, converged almost exactly with IAS 7, with prominent Indian relevance for working capital cycles and RBI reporting.

## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IAS 7

Ind AS 7 reproduces IAS 7's cash flow classification and financing activities reconciliation requirements almost exactly. Its Indian practical relevance is prominent given RBI's interest in cash flow disclosures for banks/NBFCs. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
