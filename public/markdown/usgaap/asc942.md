# ASC 942 – Financial Services–Depository and Lending

Governs loan origination fees, allowance for credit losses, core deposit intangibles, and loan modifications for depository and lending institutions.

ASC 942 addresses loan origination fees and costs with yield amortization methods, cross-references to ASC 326 for credit losses, post-ASU 2022-02 loan modification guidance, and core deposit intangibles. Key differences with IFRS for depository and lending institutions are noted.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** Divergence from IFRS 9

ASC 942's loan modification and credit loss models differ from IFRS 9's expected credit loss approach. Core deposit intangible treatment and loan origination cost deferral also diverge from IFRS norms.
