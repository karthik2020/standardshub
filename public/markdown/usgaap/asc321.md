# Investments–Equity Securities

Governs equity securities measurement, primarily at fair value through net income, introduced by ASU 2016-01 effective 2018.

ASC 321 governs equity securities with readily determinable fair values, requiring measurement at fair value through net income, with the measurement alternative for non-readily-determinable fair value equity securities.
