# Preface to International Public Sector Accounting Standards

Sets out the objectives, scope, publication suite, authority, equal authority of bold/plain type paragraphs, dual accrual/cash development tracks, and voluntary adoption framework of the IPSASB.

The Preface to International Public Sector Accounting Standards explains the institutional origin and public interest objective of the IPSASB. It defines the scope of entities to which IPSAS apply (non-profit public sector service providers), establishes the equal authority of bold and plain text paragraphs, outlines the two accrual development tracks (converged with IFRS vs public-sector-specific), explains the transitional path from cash to accrual accounting, and notes the voluntary nature of adoption across jurisdictions.
