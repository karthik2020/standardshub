# ASC 920 – Entertainment–Broadcasters

Governs program license agreements, amortization of program rights, and barter transactions for entertainment broadcasters.

ASC 920 addresses the accounting for program license agreements, four recognition criteria patterns, amortization of program rights, barter transactions, and network affiliation agreements. It notes that no dedicated IFRS broadcaster standard exists.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## History & Development

History identifier: `asc920`

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No dedicated IFRS broadcaster standard exists. While general IFRS standards on revenue and intangible assets apply, ASC 920's industry-specific program license guidance has no direct counterpart.
