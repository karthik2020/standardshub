# Compensation–General

Governs compensated absences, including the four-part accrual test for vacation, sick leave, and other paid time off.

ASC 710 governs compensation generally, including compensated absences such as vacation, sick leave, and other paid time off, using a four-part accrual test determining when liability must be recognised.
