# Limited Liability Entities

Governs financial reporting for limited liability entities such as LLCs and LLPs, addressing members' equity presentation and disclosure requirements.

ASC 272 addresses financial reporting for limited liability entities such as limited liability companies (LLCs) and limited liability partnerships (LLPs), including members' equity presentation and related disclosures.
