## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IAS 36

Ind AS 36 reproduces IAS 36's recoverable amount, cash-generating unit (CGU), and goodwill impairment testing framework almost exactly, with no substantive divergence. Its Indian practical relevance is prominent for the discount rate benchmarks used in value-in-use calculations and for CGU determination in large, diversified Indian conglomerate structures. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
