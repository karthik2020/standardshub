# Accounting Responses to Changing Prices

A transitional standard responding to the high-inflation environment of the 1970s, encouraging (but not mandating) supplementary disclosures reflecting the effects of changing prices. Withdrawn in 1989, replaced in substance by IAS 15 and later IAS 29.
