# ASC 965 – Plan Accounting–Health and Welfare Benefit Plans

Governs plan-level health and welfare benefit plan reporting including benefit obligations and claims incurred but not reported.

ASC 965 addresses health and welfare benefit plan reporting, including statements of net assets available for benefits and changes in net assets and benefit obligations, benefit obligations for postretirement health and welfare benefits, and claims incurred but not reported liabilities. It has narrower IFRS correspondence than ASC 960 and 962.

**Issued:** 1974-07

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No direct IFRS equivalent exists for ASC 965's specific health and welfare plan reporting guidance. IFRS lacks a standard with the same narrow focus on postretirement benefit plan accounting.
