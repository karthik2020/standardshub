# Joint Arrangements

Requires an entity that is a party to a joint arrangement to determine its type – joint operation or joint venture – and to account for it on that basis: a joint operation is recognised on a look-through basis (the entity's share of assets, liabilities, revenue and expenses), while a joint venture continues to use the equity method. Effective 1 January 2017. (IFRS 11 equivalent; supersedes IPSAS 8.)

IPSAS 37 Joint Arrangements requires a public-sector entity that is a party to a joint arrangement to classify the arrangement as a joint operation or a joint venture and to account for it accordingly. A joint operation is recognised on a look-through basis – the entity recognises its share of the assets, liabilities, revenue and expenses – while a joint venture continues to be accounted for using the equity method. The Standard covers the unanimous-consent joint-control test, the operation-vs-venture distinction, and transitional provisions.
