# Income Statement–Reporting Comprehensive Income

Governs the reporting and presentation of other comprehensive income, including presentation options and reclassification adjustments.

ASC 220 governs how other comprehensive income is reported and presented, including the choice between a single-statement and two-statement approach, and the disclosure of reclassification adjustments.
