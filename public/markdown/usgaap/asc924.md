# ASC 924 – Entertainment–Casinos

Governs accounting for chip liabilities, progressive jackpots, and complimentary services in casino operations.

ASC 924 addresses outstanding gaming chip liabilities, progressive jackpot accruals distinguishing base from progressive components, accounting for complimentary services, and casino credit. It provides worked examples for jackpot liability and comp allocation. No dedicated IFRS casino standard exists.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No dedicated IFRS casino standard exists. ASC 924's industry-specific guidance on chip liabilities, jackpots, and comps has no direct IFRS counterpart.
