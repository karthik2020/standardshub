# Disclosure of Financial Information about the General Government Sector

Prescribes disclosure of financial information about the general government sector (GGS), including statistical-sector classification (GGS, PFC, PNFC), reconciliations between statistical and IPSAS bases, and an illustrative GGS statement of financial position. Effective 1 January 2008.

IPSAS 22 Disclosure of Financial Information about the General Government Sector prescribes how to present general government sector financial information, including statistical-sector classification and reconciliations.
