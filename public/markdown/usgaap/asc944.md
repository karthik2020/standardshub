# ASC 944 – Financial Services–Insurance

Addresses short-duration and long-duration insurance contract accounting, including premium revenue, loss reserves, and deferred acquisition costs.

ASC 944 covers short-duration contract premium revenue and loss reserve models, traditional long-duration contract net premium reserve methods, and targeted improvements from ASU 2018-12 (LDTI). It fundamentally diverges from IFRS 17's fulfillment cash flow plus CSM measurement architecture.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** Divergence from IFRS 17

ASC 944's net premium reserve and deferred acquisition cost models differ fundamentally from IFRS 17's building-block approach using fulfillment cash flows and contractual service margin.
