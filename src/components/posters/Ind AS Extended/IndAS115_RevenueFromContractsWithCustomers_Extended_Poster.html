Conceptual Framework Linkage

Identical control-based recognition philosophy to IFRS 15
Ind AS 115 applies the IDENTICAL core principle as IFRS 15 – revenue is recognised to depict the transfer of promised goods/services to customers in an amount reflecting the CONSIDERATION the entity expects to be entitled to, replacing the old risks-and-rewards-based approach with a CONTROL-based five-step model.
HOW IND AS 115 MAPS TO THE FRAMEWORK
Faithful representation – depicting the genuine pattern of value transfer to customers – identical to IFRS 15Comparability – a single revenue model replacing the numerous prior industry-specific standards (construction contracts, real estate guidance) – identical to IFRS 15Cost constraint for smaller Indian entities – India provides certain DISCLOSURE-LEVEL relaxations for companies whose debt/equity instruments are NOT listed, reducing the burden of some of Ind AS 115's more extensive disaggregated revenue disclosures for this population – a genuinely distinctive Indian cost-benefit calibration
Ind AS 115's RECOGNITION AND MEASUREMENT principles carry over from IFRS 15 without substantive modification – the meaningful Indian difference sits entirely in the DISCLOSURE layer for certain unlisted entities (Section 7).

Scope & Applicability

What Ind AS 115 covers
Ind AS 115 applies to ALL contracts with customers except leases, insurance contracts, financial instruments, and certain non-monetary exchanges – IDENTICAL scope to IFRS 15.
Ind AS 115 REPLACED the earlier Ind AS 11 (Construction Contracts) and Ind AS 18 (Revenue), identical in effect to how IFRS 15 replaced IAS 11 and IAS 18 globally.
Key definitions you must know
Performance obligation
IDENTICAL definition to IFRS 15 – a promise to transfer a distinct good or service.
Unlisted company (Ind AS context)
A company whose debt/equity securities are NOT listed on a recognised stock exchange, eligible for certain Ind AS 115 disclosure relaxations.

Identifying Performance Obligations (with worked example)

Identical distinctness criteria to IFRS 15
Example 1: An Indian software company's bundled contract
An Indian SaaS company sells a bundled contract for software licence access (₹6,00,000) plus one year of implementation support (₹1,50,000) and ongoing maintenance (₹2,50,000), each capable of being sold separately and used independently by the customer.
EACH component is DISTINCT and represents a SEPARATE performance obligation, requiring the ₹10,00,000 total transaction price to be ALLOCATED across all three based on standalone selling prices – an IDENTICAL analysis to how IFRS 15 would treat the same bundled software contract globally.

Recognising Revenue – Point in Time vs Over Time

Identical three-criteria over-time test to IFRS 15
The over-time recognition criteria (customer simultaneously receives/consumes benefits; entity's performance creates/enhances a customer-controlled asset; or the asset has no alternative use PLUS an enforceable right to payment) are IDENTICAL to IFRS 15 – relevant for Indian real estate and EPC/construction sector revenue recognition, replacing the old Ind AS 11 percentage-of-completion method with this new control-based framework.
7

Disclosure Relaxations for Unlisted Indian Companies (with worked example)

The genuinely important Indian divergence point
Ind AS 115 provides RELIEF from certain extensive disclosure requirements (e.g. some disaggregated revenue disclosures, remaining performance obligation disclosures) for companies whose debt/equity instruments are NOT listed on a stock exchange – a COST-BENEFIT calibration NOT present in IFRS 15 for a comparably-sized global entity population, which generally applies its disclosure requirements more uniformly regardless of listing status.
Example 2: Disclosure burden for a listed vs unlisted Indian company
An Indian LISTED manufacturing company and an otherwise-comparable UNLISTED Indian manufacturing company both apply Ind AS 115's five-step recognition model identically to their sales contracts.
The LISTED company must provide the FULL suite of Ind AS 115 disclosures (disaggregated revenue, contract balances, remaining performance obligations). The UNLISTED company may be ELIGIBLE for certain disclosure relaxations under the applicable Ind AS rules – while its underlying REVENUE RECOGNITION accounting remains IDENTICAL to the listed company's and to IFRS 15's own model.

Contract Costs

Identical to IFRS 15
Incremental costs of obtaining a contract, and costs to fulfil a contract, are capitalised and amortised on a systematic basis consistent with the transfer of goods/services – IDENTICAL to IFRS 15.
