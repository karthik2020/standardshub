# Cost of Sales and Services

Governs cost of sales and services presentation, including gross profit presentation, freight, and installation costs.

ASC 705 governs cost of sales and services, including the classification and presentation of costs of goods sold and costs of services, as well as specific guidance on freight, installation, and related costs.
