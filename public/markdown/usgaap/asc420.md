# Exit or Disposal Cost Obligations

Governs one-time termination benefits and other exit/disposal cost obligations, with recognition upon commitment rather than upon consumption of benefits.

ASC 420 governs exit or disposal cost obligations, including one-time termination benefits and other costs associated with a restructuring, recognised when the obligation is incurred.
