# Income Taxes

Governs income taxes using the asset/liability method, including deferred tax assets/liabilities, valuation allowances, and uncertain tax positions.

ASC 740 governs income taxes, applying the asset/liability method to account for deferred taxes based on temporary differences, requiring valuation allowances for deferred tax assets and recognition of uncertain tax positions under the more-likely-than-not threshold.
