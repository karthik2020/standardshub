# ASC 970 – Real Estate–General

Governs preacquisition costs, development costs, interest capitalization, and selling cost capitalization for real estate projects.

ASC 970 addresses preacquisition cost capitalization criteria, land development and construction costs, capitalization of interest and taxes during development, selling costs capitalizable only if directly related and recoverable, and cost allocation for multiple-unit real estate projects. Real estate sales are now governed by ASC 606, and it is broadly converged with IFRS post-IFRS 15.
