## Notice

**success:** Substantially converged with IAS/IFRS

Ind AS 111 reproduces IFRS 11's joint operation vs joint venture classification almost exactly. Its most common Indian application is in infrastructure and construction consortium arrangements, and oil/gas exploration joint ventures.
