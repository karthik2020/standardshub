# Employee Benefits

Withdrawn standard. IPSAS 25 Employee Benefits was superseded for periods beginning 1 January 2018 by IPSAS 39 Employee Benefits, which reflects the IASB's revised IAS 19 (2011) – notably eliminating the corridor approach for deferred actuarial gains/losses. Retained as a numbering placeholder with a redirect map.

IPSAS 25 Employee Benefits was withdrawn; superseded by IPSAS 39 (effective periods beginning 1 January 2018). Retained as a numbering placeholder.
