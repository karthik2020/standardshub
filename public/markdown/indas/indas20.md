# Accounting for Government Grants and Disclosure of Government Assistance

Prescribes the accounting for government grants and disclosure of government assistance, converged with IAS 20, with a genuine presentation divergence: the deduction-from-asset method is prohibited.

## Notice

**warning:** Ind AS – Converged with IAS 20, with a Genuine Presentation Divergence

IAS 20 permits grants related to assets to be presented EITHER as deferred income OR as a deduction from the asset's carrying amount. Ind AS 20 REMOVES the deduction option – grants related to assets MUST be presented as DEFERRED INCOME only. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
