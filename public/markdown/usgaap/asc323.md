# Investments–Equity Method and Joint Ventures

Governs the equity method of accounting for investments in associates and joint ventures, including the 20–50% rebuttable presumption and elimination of unrealised intercompany profits.

ASC 323 governs the equity method of accounting for investments in associates and joint ventures, including the 20–50% ownership presumption, unrealised intercompany profit elimination, and joint venture accounting.
