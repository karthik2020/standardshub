# Investments–Other

Governs investments in insurance contracts and beneficial interests in securitised financial assets; a narrowed residual Topic since ASU 2016-01.

ASC 325 governs investments in insurance contracts and beneficial interests in securitized financial assets, a narrowed residual Topic since the cost method guidance moved to ASC 321 under ASU 2016-01.
