# Changing Prices

Addresses supplementary information showing the effects of changing prices on financial statements; largely dormant with voluntary requirements since SFAS 89 (1986).

ASC 255 addresses supplementary information showing the effects of changing prices on financial statements – specifically, constant purchasing power and current cost restatements. Its substantive disclosure requirements were made voluntary by SFAS 89 in 1986 and are essentially unused today.
