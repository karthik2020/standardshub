# ASC 958 – Not-for-Profit Entities

Establishes net asset classification, contribution recognition, and functional expense reporting for not-for-profit entities.

ASC 958 governs net asset classification between with and without donor restrictions, contribution recognition distinguishing unconditional from conditional promises, the scoping of contributions out of ASC 606, donated services and gifts-in-kind, and functional expense reporting by nature and function. No dedicated IFRS not-for-profit standard exists.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No dedicated IFRS not-for-profit standard exists. ASC 958's donor-restriction classifications and functional expense matrices have no direct IFRS counterpart.
