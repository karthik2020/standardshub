# ASC 962 – Plan Accounting–Defined Contribution Pension Plans

Governs plan-level defined contribution pension plan reporting including participant-directed investment programs.

ASC 962 addresses plan-level reporting for defined contribution pension plans, covering the statement of net assets available for benefits, participant-directed versus nonparticipant-directed investment programs, and participant account allocations. It is the counterpart of IAS 26 for defined contribution plans.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**info:** Converged with IAS 26

ASC 962 aligns closely with IAS 26 for defined contribution plan reporting. Both standards require statements of net assets available for benefits and changes in net assets, without actuarial obligation measurement.
