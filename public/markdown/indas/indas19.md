## Notice

**warning:** Ind AS – Converged with IAS 19, with a Genuine Plan-Classification Nuance

Ind AS 19's defined benefit measurement model (projected unit credit method, OCI remeasurements) mirrors IAS 19 closely. The distinctive Indian consideration is that EPFO-administered Provident Fund contributions, which carry a GOVERNMENT INTEREST RATE GUARANTEE, may require DEFINED BENEFIT treatment rather than the simpler defined contribution treatment – a genuinely important classification nuance for Indian employers. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
