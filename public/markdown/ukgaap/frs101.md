# FRS 101 – Reduced Disclosure Framework | Extended Reference

FRS 101 provides a reduced disclosure framework for qualifying entities within an IFRS group, allowing them to use full IFRS recognition and measurement with disclosure exemptions.

FRS 101 applies to the individual financial statements of qualifying entities that are members of a group preparing publicly available consolidated IFRS accounts. It exempts entities from many detailed IFRS disclosure requirements, including cash flow statements, IFRS 7 financial instrument risk disclosures, IFRS 8 segment reporting, and certain related party disclosures, while keeping recognition and measurement identical to full IFRS. Unlike US GAAP's private company council alternatives, FRS 101 is purely a disclosure relief mechanism rather than a separate measurement basis.
