# Debt

Governs debt including issuance costs netted against the liability, current/noncurrent classification, debt modifications/extinguishments, and conversion features.

ASC 470 governs debt, including initial measurement, debt issuance cost presentation, classification as current or noncurrent, modifications and extinguishments, and troubled debt restructurings by debtors.
