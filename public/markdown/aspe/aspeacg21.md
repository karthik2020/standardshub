# Accounting for Life Insurance Contracts with Cash Surrender Value

Accounting for Life Insurance Contracts with Cash Surrender Value is part of the Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises (ASPE).

This standard is part of the ASPE framework issued by the Canadian Accounting Standards Board. It provides guidance on accounting for life insurance contracts with cash surrender value.
