# Intangible Assets

Prescribes recognition and measurement of intangible assets held for use in the public sector – including the IPSAS-specific three-part intangible-asset test, separate versus combination acquisition, internally generated assets (research vs development), and the choice between historical-cost and current-value models. Effective 1 April 2011. (IAS 38 equivalent, adapted for the public sector.)

IPSAS 31 Intangible Assets prescribes the accounting for intangible assets controlled by public sector entities, recognising them when it is probable that future economic benefits will flow and the cost or value can be measured reliably. It sets out an IPSAS-specific three-part intangible-asset test, distinguishes acquisition recognised separately from acquisition in a combination, addresses internally generated intangibles (research versus development), permits measurement under the historical-cost or current-value model, and requires amortisation over a finite useful life (or assessment for indefinite life) with impairment testing.
