## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IAS 33

Ind AS 33 reproduces IAS 33's basic/diluted EPS calculation mechanics almost exactly, with no substantive divergence. Its Indian practical relevance is prominent given widespread CCPS (Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares) and ESOP (Employee Stock Option Plan) dilutive instrument structures. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
