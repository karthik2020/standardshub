# Other Expenses

Governs other expenses not covered by more specific Topics, including start-up costs, advertising, and restructuring costs.

ASC 720 governs other expenses, including start-up costs, advertising costs, restructuring costs, and other miscellaneous operating expenses not addressed by more specific Topics.
