# Introduction to the Cash Basis IPSAS

Provides the background, purpose, jurisdictional autonomy principles, and binary compliance threshold for the Cash Basis IPSAS, framing it as an interim stepping stone toward accrual adoption.

The Introduction to the Cash Basis IPSAS serves as a preamble to the 2017 revised Standard. It explains why a cash-basis standard exists alongside accrual IPSAS, respects the sovereignty of national standard-setters, reinforces that compliance requires satisfying all of Part 1, and encourages governments to use the standard as a path toward accrual-basis IPSAS and Study 14 transition guidance.
