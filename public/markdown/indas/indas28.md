## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IAS 28

Ind AS 28 reproduces IAS 28's equity method mechanics and significant influence test almost exactly, with no substantive divergence. Its Indian practical relevance is prominent given the widespread use of joint venture/associate structures in Indian conglomerates and infrastructure groups. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
