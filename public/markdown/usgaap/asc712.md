# Nonretirement Postemployment Benefits

Governs postemployment benefits other than retirement, including health care, life insurance, and disability benefits.

ASC 712 governs nonretirement postemployment benefits, including postemployment health care, life insurance, and disability benefits, distinguishing between vesting/accumulating benefits and other benefit arrangements.
