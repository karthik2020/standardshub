## Notice

**warning:** Ind AS – Converged with IAS 1, but with a Genuine Format-Level Divergence

Ind AS 1's core presentation principles mirror IAS 1 closely, but Indian companies MUST also comply with Schedule III of the Companies Act, 2013, which PRESCRIBES a specific balance sheet/P&L format that IAS 1 does not mandate globally. Ind AS 1 also REQUIRES a single combined statement of profit and loss (P&L + OCI), removing the two-statement option IAS 1 permits. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
