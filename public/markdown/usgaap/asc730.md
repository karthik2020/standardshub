# Research and Development

Governs research and development costs, requiring all R&D to be expensed as incurred, with specific guidance on software development costs.

ASC 730 governs research and development costs, requiring all research and development costs to be expensed as incurred, with specific guidance on capitalisation of software development costs after technological feasibility.
