## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IAS 32

Ind AS 32's liability/equity classification principles, compound instrument split-accounting, and offsetting criteria mirror IAS 32 almost exactly, with no substantive divergence. Its Indian practical relevance is prominent given the widespread use of Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS) and redeemable preference shares in Indian startup/family business funding structures. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
