Conceptual Framework Linkage

Identical equity method philosophy to IAS 28
Ind AS 28 applies the IDENTICAL principle as IAS 28 — investments in associates (SIGNIFICANT INFLUENCE, not control) and joint ventures (JOINT CONTROL, classified as a JV under Ind AS 111) are accounted for using the EQUITY METHOD, recognising the investor's SHARE of the investee's profit/loss.
HOW IND AS 28 MAPS TO THE FRAMEWORK
Faithful representation — the equity method reflects the investor's economic interest in the investee's net assets/performance more faithfully than a simple cost-based approach — identical to IAS 28Widespread Indian application — Indian business groups (conglomerates) and infrastructure companies FREQUENTLY hold associate/JV stakes as part of their corporate structuring, making Ind AS 28's equity method GENUINELY significant in Indian practice
Ind AS 28 introduced NO substantive change to the equity method relative to IAS 28 — its Indian relevance is most pronounced for the SCALE and FREQUENCY of associate/JV holdings among Indian business groups.
The significant influence presumption — identical to IAS 28
Holding ≥ 20% of voting power = REBUTTABLE presumption of significant influence
ℹ️ IDENTICAL threshold and rebuttable nature to IAS 28.
2

Scope & Applicability

What Ind AS 28 covers
Ind AS 28 applies to accounting for investments in associates and joint ventures, and prescribes the equity method's application — IDENTICAL scope to IAS 28.
Key definitions you must know
Significant influence
IDENTICAL definition to IAS 28 — the power to participate in financial/operating policy decisions, without control or joint control.
Equity method
IDENTICAL definition to IAS 28 — initial recognition at cost, adjusted for the investor's post-acquisition share of the investee's profit/loss and OCI.
3

Significant Influence — The 20% Threshold (with worked example)

Identical rebuttable presumption to IAS 28
Example 1: Significant influence despite a sub-20% holding
An Indian company holds only 15% of an investee's voting shares but has a contractual right to appoint ONE director to the investee's board and participates in key policy-making decisions.
Despite the SUB-20% holding, significant influence likely EXISTS due to board representation and participation in policy decisions — an IDENTICAL rebuttable presumption analysis to how IAS 28 would assess the same fact pattern globally.
4

The Equity Method — Initial & Subsequent Measurement

Identical mechanics to IAS 28
Initial recognition at cost; subsequently ADJUSTED for the investor's share of profit/loss (through P&L) and OCI (through OCI), with dividends received REDUCING the carrying amount — IDENTICAL to IAS 28.
5

Losses Exceeding the Investment

Identical to IAS 28
Once the investment's carrying amount reaches ZERO, further losses are recognised ONLY to the extent the investor has incurred legal/constructive obligations or made payments on the investee's behalf — IDENTICAL to IAS 28.
6

Impairment of Equity-Accounted Investments

Identical to IAS 28
The ENTIRE carrying amount of the investment (not underlying assets separately) is tested for impairment under Ind AS 36's recoverable amount framework when indicators exist — IDENTICAL to IAS 28.

Indian Conglomerate & Infrastructure JV Context

A practically prominent Indian application area
Large Indian business groups frequently structure cross-holdings, strategic partnerships, and infrastructure/manufacturing joint ventures involving SIGNIFICANT INFLUENCE (rather than control) stakes — making Ind AS 28's equity method GENUINELY significant across sectors including telecom, cement, automotive, and financial services, where minority strategic stakes and joint venture structures (often with foreign partners) are common.
8

Discontinuing the Equity Method

Identical to IAS 28
The equity method ceases from the date significant influence/joint control is LOST, with the RETAINED interest remeasured at fair value — IDENTICAL to IAS 28.
