# Borrowing Costs

Prescribes the accounting treatment for borrowing costs. Generally requires immediate expensing, but PERMITS – as an allowed alternative – capitalizing borrowing costs directly attributable to acquiring, constructing, or producing a qualifying asset. Covers the benchmark and allowed-alternative treatments, eligible costs for specific and general borrowings, and commencement, suspension, and cessation of capitalization.

IPSAS 5 Borrowing Costs prescribes the accounting treatment for borrowing costs. Generally it requires that borrowing costs be recognized as an expense in the period in which they are incurred, but it permits, as an allowed alternative treatment, the capitalization of borrowing costs directly attributable to acquiring, constructing, or producing a qualifying asset.
