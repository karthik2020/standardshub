Conceptual Framework Linkage

The companion standard applying the general model to reinsurance
ASBE No. 26 governs REINSURANCE contracts — BOTH reinsurance HELD (CEDED to ANOTHER reinsurer) and reinsurance ISSUED (ASSUMED from ANOTHER insurer) — APPLYING the SAME general BUILDING block MEASUREMENT model INTRODUCED for DIRECT insurance CONTRACTS under ASBE No. 25 (see the COMPANION poster), ADAPTED for the SPECIFIC fact PATTERNS reinsurance ARRANGEMENTS present.
2

Scope — Reinsurance Held and Reinsurance Issued

Both sides of a reinsurance arrangement
This Standard applies to REINSURANCE contracts an INSURER HOLDS (as a CEDING company TRANSFERRING risk to a REINSURER) and REINSURANCE contracts an INSURER ISSUES (as a REINSURER ACCEPTING risk from ANOTHER insurer) — COVERING BOTH sides of the REINSURANCE relationship.
3

Reinsurance Contracts Held

A separate asset, measured using the same building block principles
A REINSURANCE contract HELD is RECOGNISED as a SEPARATE asset — MEASURED using FULFILMENT cash FLOWS and a CONTRACTUAL service MARGIN, PARALLELING the GENERAL model — RATHER than SIMPLY netted AGAINST the UNDERLYING direct INSURANCE liabilities it RELATES to.
4

Recognition Timing — Aligned With the Underlying Contracts

Recognised no later than the underlying loss-making group
A REINSURANCE contract HELD is RECOGNISED WHEN the UNDERLYING insurance CONTRACTS it RELATES to are RECOGNISED, OR — WHERE EARLIER — WHEN the UNDERLYING group of CONTRACTS becomes ONEROUS (loss-MAKING), triggering EARLIER recognition of the REINSURANCE asset'S offsetting BENEFIT.
5

Reinsurance Contracts Issued (Assumed)

Measured consistently with direct insurance contracts issued
WHERE an ENTERPRISE ISSUES (ASSUMES) reinsurance CONTRACTS, THEY are MEASURED CONSISTENTLY with HOW the SAME entity WOULD measure a DIRECT insurance contract ISSUED under ASBE No. 25 — the SAME building BLOCK approach, ADAPTED for the SPECIFIC terms of the REINSURANCE arrangement.
6

The Same Building Block Model, Applied to Reinsurance

Fulfilment cash flows and a contractual service margin, from the reinsurer's perspective
CONSISTENT with ASBE No. 25, MEASUREMENT is BUILT from FULFILMENT cash FLOWS (probability-WEIGHTED, discounted, PLUS a risk ADJUSTMENT) and a CONTRACTUAL service MARGIN — REPRESENTING, for a REINSURANCE contract HELD, the NET COST or gain of PURCHASING reinsurance PROTECTION, recognised OVER the period REINSURANCE coverage is RECEIVED.
7

Why a Separate Standard, Rather Than One Unified Standard

A structural choice, not a substantive divergence
AS discussed in the COMPANION ASBE No. 25 poster, IFRS 17 addresses BOTH direct INSURANCE and REINSURANCE within a SINGLE, unified STANDARD, WHEREAS China SPLITS the EQUIVALENT guidance across TWO separate STANDARDS. This REFLECTS a GENERAL ASBE preference for SEPARATING distinct TOPIC areas into THEIR own dedicated STANDARDS, RATHER than a SUBSTANTIVE difference in the UNDERLYING measurement PRINCIPLES themselves.
8

Worked Example — Recognising a Reinsurance Contract Held

A separate asset, measured on the building block basis
Example: Ceding a portion of catastrophe risk
An insurer purchases reinsurance protection for a portfolio of property policies, ceding 40% of the underlying risk. The reinsurance contract's fulfilment cash flows and CSM are measured following the general building block model.
The insurer recognises a SEPARATE reinsurance CONTRACT asset, MEASURED using the SAME fulfilment CASH flow and CSM MECHANICS as its DIRECT insurance liabilities — RECOGNISED no LATER than the UNDERLYING direct policies THEMSELVES are recognised.
9

Worked Example — Early Recognition Following an Onerous Underlying Group

Recognising the offsetting benefit as soon as the loss is identified
Example: A loss-making underlying policy group triggers early recognition
A group of underlying direct insurance contracts becomes onerous (loss-making) before the reinsurance contract covering them would otherwise be recognised under the general timing rule.
The INSURER recognises the REINSURANCE contract HELD ASSET EARLY — AT the SAME time the UNDERLYING onerous GROUP is recognised — ENSURING the OFFSETTING benefit of the REINSURANCE protection is REFLECTED WITHOUT delay.

Quick-Reference Glossary

Reinsurance contract held
A reinsurance arrangement in which the reporting entity cedes risk to another reinsurer, recognised as a separate asset.
Onerous group
A group of insurance contracts expected to be loss-making, triggering early recognition of a related reinsurance contract held.
