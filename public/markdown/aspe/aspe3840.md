# Related Party Transactions

Related Party Transactions is part of the Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises (ASPE).

This standard is part of the ASPE framework issued by the Canadian Accounting Standards Board. It provides guidance on related party transactions.

**Issued:** 2006-02

**Latest Amendment:** 2016-01

**Effective:** 2006-02-01

## History & Development

History identifier: `aspe3840`

## Notice

**warning:** Prescribes Measurement, Not Just Disclosure

Unlike IAS 24, which is disclosure-only, ASPE Section 3840 actively prescribes a measurement basis – carrying amount or, in narrower circumstances, exchange amount.
