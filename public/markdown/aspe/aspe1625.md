# Comprehensive Revaluation of Assets and Liabilities

Comprehensive Revaluation of Assets and Liabilities is part of the Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises (ASPE).

This standard is part of the ASPE framework issued by the Canadian Accounting Standards Board. It provides guidance on comprehensive revaluation of assets and liabilities.
