# Leases

Requires lessees to recognise most leases on-balance-sheet via right-of-use assets and lease liabilities, substantially converged with IFRS 16, with an India-specific COVID-19 rent concession practical expedient.

## Notice

**success:** Substantially converged with IAS/IFRS

Ind AS 116 reproduces IFRS 16's single lessee on-balance-sheet model almost exactly, including identical recognition exemptions. India also introduced its own COVID-19 rent concession practical expedient via a separate MCA amendment.
