DRS 2 (+ DRS 2-10, DRS 2-20) — Kapitalflussrechnung
Cash flow statement — general standard, plus sector-specific variants for credit institutions (2-10) and insurance undertakings (2-20)
⛔ WITHDRAWN 2014 — replaced by DRS 21
Part of the DRS consolidation wave
DRS 2 was the DRSC's original, general cash flow statement standard, elaborating the Kapitalflussrechnung requirement established under §297(1) HGB (covered in the earlier HGB poster series). Reflecting how differently a bank's or insurer's own cash flows are structured compared to an ordinary trading/manufacturing enterprise, the DRSC ALSO issued two dedicated sector-specific companion standards: DRS 2-10 for credit institutions, and DRS 2-20 for insurance undertakings — a genuinely distinctive three-standard cluster covering the same underlying statement across different industries.
Why withdrawn — replaced by a single, unified successor
The BMJ formally withdrew DRS 2 (and its two sector companions) on 8 April 2014, with the Bundesanzeiger announcement of the new DRS 21 "Kapitalflussrechnung" — a single, UNIFIED cash flow statement standard that folded the general rules AND the credit-institution/insurance-specific adaptations into ONE document (using dedicated internal annexes for the sector-specific content, rather than entirely separate standalone standards). Last applicable to fiscal years beginning on or before 31 December 2014.
