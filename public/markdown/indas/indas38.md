## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IAS 38

Ind AS 38 reproduces IAS 38's research/development split, recognition criteria, and amortisation model almost exactly, with no substantive divergence. Its Indian practical relevance is prominent for the IT/software and pharmaceutical sectors' R&D capitalisation judgements. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
