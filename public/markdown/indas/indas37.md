## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IAS 37

Ind AS 37 reproduces IAS 37's provisions recognition criteria and contingent liability/asset framework almost exactly, with no substantive divergence. Its Indian practical relevance is prominent given the volume and duration of Indian litigation and tax disputes. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
