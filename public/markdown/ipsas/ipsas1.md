# Presentation of Financial Statements

Prescribes the basis for presenting general purpose financial statements prepared under the accrual basis by public sector entities, covering structure, minimum content, fair presentation, the complete set of statements, going concern, current/non-current classification, comparative information, and related disclosures.

IPSAS 1 Presentation of Financial Statements prescribes the overall requirements for presenting general purpose financial statements prepared under the accrual basis by public sector entities, including structure, minimum content, fair presentation, the complete set of statements, going concern, current/non-current classification, comparative information, and related-party and other disclosures.
