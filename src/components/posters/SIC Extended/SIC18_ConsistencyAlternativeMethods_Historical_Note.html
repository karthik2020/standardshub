SIC-18 — Consistency—Alternative Methods
Historical reference note · Issued 2000 · Withdrawn 2003
⚠ WITHDRAWN — Superseded by the 2003 Improvements project and IAS 8 (revised 2003), effective for periods beginning 1 January 2005
What SIC-18 addressed
In the era before the 2003 Improvements project, several Standards permitted a choice between a "BENCHMARK" treatment and an "ALLOWED ALTERNATIVE" treatment for the same type of transaction (for example, the original IAS 2, IAS 16, IAS 23, and IAS 38 each contained such pairs). SIC-18 addressed how CONSISTENTLY an entity had to apply its chosen treatment once selected.
The conclusion it reached
Where a Standard or Interpretation permitted a choice between a benchmark and an allowed alternative treatment, the entity had to select ONE treatment as its ACCOUNTING POLICY and apply it CONSISTENTLY to all similar transactions — unless a specific Standard or Interpretation itself required or permitted categorisation of items for which genuinely different policies might be appropriate.
Where this guidance lives today
Superseded byThe 2003 Improvements project (which eliminated most benchmark/allowed-alternative treatment pairs) and IAS 8 (revised 2003)
Why withdrawnThe IASB's 2003 Improvements project removed the "allowed alternative" concept from almost all affected standards, largely eliminating the choice SIC-18 was designed to govern; the surviving general consistency principle was absorbed into IAS 8
Current statusLargely obsolete — very few IFRSs today offer a genuine benchmark/allowed-alternative choice of the kind SIC-18 addressed, though IAS 8's general consistency-of-accounting-policy principle remains a live requirement
