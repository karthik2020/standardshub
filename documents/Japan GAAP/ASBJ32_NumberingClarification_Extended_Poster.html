ASBJ Guidance No. 32 — Implementation Guidance on Accounting Standard for Semi-annual Financial Reporting
Extended reference poster · Japan GAAP (J-GAAP) · A numbering clarification, plus companion Guidance to Statement No. 33 · JPY examples
NUMBERING CLARIFICATION: There is no separately numbered "ASBJ Statement No. 32." Within ASBJ's parallel numbering systems for Statements and Implementation Guidance, "No. 32" in this position corresponds to ASBJ Guidance No. 32 — the Implementation Guidance issued alongside ASBJ Statement No. 33 (Semi-annual Financial Reporting) in March 2024. This poster explains that companion guidance and the numbering structure itself.

Conceptual Framework Linkage

Implementation Guidance interprets and operationalises a Statement
ASBJ's output is organised into SEVERAL parallel document TYPES — "ACCOUNTING STANDARDS" (ASBJ Statements, the PRIMARY, principle-setting documents), "IMPLEMENTATION GUIDANCE" (detailed, PRACTICAL guidance interpreting and applying a Statement's requirements), "PRACTICAL SOLUTIONS" (narrower, often transitional interpretive guidance), and "TRANSFERRED GUIDANCE" (older interpretive material CARRIED FORWARD from a predecessor body). EACH document type has its OWN, SEPARATE numbering sequence — meaning "No. 32" can refer to DIFFERENT documents depending on WHICH sequence is being referenced.
2

ASBJ's Parallel Numbering Systems — Statements vs Implementation Guidance

Two independently numbered sequences running side by side
ACCOUNTING STANDARDS (ASBJ Statements) are numbered 1, 2, 3... independently from IMPLEMENTATION GUIDANCE (ASBJ Guidance), which is ALSO numbered 1, 2, 3... in its OWN, SEPARATE sequence. A Statement and its COMPANION Guidance are OFTEN issued together but do NOT necessarily share the SAME number — for example, ASBJ Statement No. 33 (Semi-annual Financial Reporting) was issued ALONGSIDE ASBJ Guidance No. 32 (its companion Implementation Guidance), NOT "ASBJ Statement No. 32."
3

Scope of ASBJ Guidance No. 32

Practical application guidance for semi-annual reporting
ASBJ Guidance No. 32 provided DETAILED, PRACTICAL guidance for applying ASBJ Statement No. 33 — the ORIGINAL 2024 Semi-annual Financial Reporting standard (see the companion poster) — addressing SPECIFIC application questions that arise in PREPARING semi-annual (second-quarter) statutory financial statements.
4

What This Guidance Provided

Practical detail supporting the principles set out in Statement No. 33
Consistent with the GENERAL role of Implementation Guidance across the ASBJ suite, this Guidance SUPPLIED practical, OPERATIONAL detail — illustrative examples, specific application scenarios, and INTERPRETIVE clarifications — supporting COMPANIES in applying Statement No. 33's PRINCIPLES-BASED semi-annual reporting requirements CONSISTENTLY.
5

Superseded by the Interim Financial Reporting Package (2025)

Reworked alongside its parent Statement
When ASBJ Statement No. 33 was REWORKED into ASBJ Statement No. 37 (Interim Financial Reporting) in October 2025 (see the companion poster), its COMPANION Implementation Guidance was LIKEWISE reworked into ASBJ Implementation Guidance No. 34 — meaning ASBJ Guidance No. 32's PRACTICAL content has been LARGELY carried forward and updated WITHIN the current Implementation Guidance No. 34, rather than remaining separately in force under its ORIGINAL number.

Worked Example — Locating a Cross-Referenced Guidance Number

Resolving a numbering ambiguity in practice
Example: A preparer encountering a reference to "No. 32"
A preparer researching Japan's semi-annual/interim reporting history encounters a reference to "No. 32" without further qualification.
Given the SURROUNDING context (semi-annual reporting, issued alongside Statement No. 33 in March 2024), the preparer can conclude this refers to ASBJ GUIDANCE No. 32 — the Implementation Guidance companion to Statement No. 33 — NOT a separately numbered Accounting Standard, resolving the AMBIGUITY by recognising ASBJ's TWO parallel numbering sequences.

Relationship to IAS/IFRS

A structural, not substantive, comparison point
The IASB's OWN standard-numbering convention (IFRS 1, 2, 3... and IAS 1, 2, 3... running as SEPARATE historical sequences) offers a ROUGH parallel to ASBJ's PARALLEL Statement/Guidance numbering — in BOTH systems, a bare NUMBER reference can be AMBIGUOUS without knowing WHICH document type or series it belongs to.
