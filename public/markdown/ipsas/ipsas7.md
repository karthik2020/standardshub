# Investments in Associates

Withdrawn standard. IPSAS 7 Investments in Associates was withdrawn as part of the IPSASB's 2015 Consolidation Suite, mirroring the IASB's 2011 revision of IAS 28 (Investments in Associates and Joint Ventures). It was superseded for annual financial statements covering periods beginning on or after 1 January 2017 by IPSAS 36 Investments in Associates and Joint Ventures. Retained as a numbering placeholder with a redirect map.

IPSAS 7 Investments in Associates prescribed the equity-method accounting for investments in associates. Withdrawn as part of the IPSASB 2015 Consolidation Suite, it was superseded for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2017 by IPSAS 36 Investments in Associates and Joint Ventures.
