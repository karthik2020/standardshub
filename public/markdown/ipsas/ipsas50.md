# Exploration for and Evaluation of Mineral Resources

Specifies financial reporting for mineral resource exploration and evaluation expenditures, exempting entities from the standard IPSAS 3 hierarchy while mandating cost measurement, tangible/intangible classification, tailored impairment indicators, and pre-reclassification impairment testing. Effective 1 January 2027.

IPSAS 50 Exploration for and Evaluation of Mineral Resources specifies the financial reporting for expenditures incurred in the exploration for and evaluation of mineral resources before technical feasibility and commercial viability are demonstrable. It exempts entities from the IPSAS 3 policy hierarchy, allows entities to determine their capitalization policies, requires initial measurement at cost and subsequent measurement under historical cost or fair value, governs tangible versus intangible classification, establishes four exploration-specific impairment trigger indicators replacing the IPSAS 26 general list, and requires mandatory impairment assessment prior to reclassifying assets upon commercial viability demonstration. Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027.
