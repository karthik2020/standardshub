# Measurement

Defines measurement bases that help fairly reflect cost of services, operational capacity, and financial capacity, covering historical cost, current operational value, cost of fulfillment, and fair value, including measurement techniques and the fair value hierarchy. Effective 1 January 2025.

IPSAS 46 Measurement defines the measurement bases that help fairly reflect cost of services, operational capacity, and financial capacity. It identifies four measurement bases – historical cost, current operational value, cost of fulfillment, and fair value – and the approaches under each basis that individual IPSAS apply. It covers initial measurement (transaction price and deemed cost), subsequent measurement models, measurement techniques (market, cost, and income approaches), depreciation and impairment, transaction costs, the fair value hierarchy (Levels 1, 2, and 3), and disclosure requirements. Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025.
