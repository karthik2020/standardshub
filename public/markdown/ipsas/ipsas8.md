# Interests in Joint Ventures

Withdrawn standard. IPSAS 8 Interests in Joint Ventures was withdrawn as part of the IPSASB's 2015 Consolidation Suite, mirroring the IASB's 2011 replacement of IAS 31 with IFRS 11. It was superseded for annual financial statements covering periods beginning on or after 1 January 2017 by IPSAS 37 Joint Arrangements. Retained as a numbering placeholder with a redirect map.

IPSAS 8 Interests in Joint Ventures prescribed accounting for interests in jointly controlled entities, including the proportionate consolidation option. Withdrawn as part of the IPSASB 2015 Consolidation Suite, it was superseded for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2017 by IPSAS 37 Joint Arrangements.
