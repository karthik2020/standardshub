ASC 605 – Revenue Recognition
US GAAP · Legacy Topic · Superseded by ASC 606 for essentially all transactions (effective 2018 public / 2019 private entities)
⚠ SUPERSEDED – ASC 606 now governs virtually all revenue recognition; ASC 605 retains almost no independent substantive content
What ASC 605 used to govern
Before the 2014 revenue recognition overhaul, ASC 605 (and its predecessor pronouncements, including SAB 101/104 and industry-specific guidance such as SOP 97-2 for software) provided the FRAMEWORK for revenue recognition under US GAAP – built around criteria such as persuasive evidence of an arrangement, delivery having occurred, a fixed or determinable price, and collectibility being reasonably assured. This framework was considerably LESS UNIFIED than the model that replaced it – it consisted of dozens of INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC and transaction-specific pieces of guidance, often producing inconsistent results for economically similar transactions across different industries.
Why it was replaced
The FASB and IASB undertook a JOINT project (beginning in the early 2000s, finalised in 2014) specifically to replace this fragmented, industry-specific patchwork with a SINGLE, PRINCIPLE-BASED, converged model – resulting in ASC 606 (US GAAP) and IFRS 15 (IFRS), issued simultaneously with substantively identical wording. ASC 606 became effective for public entities in 2018 and private entities in 2019, at which point it SUPERSEDED ASC 605 (and virtually all industry-specific revenue guidance previously scattered across the Codification) for essentially all revenue transactions.
