# Business Combinations

Prescribes the accounting for business combinations using the acquisition method, converged with IFRS 3 for most acquisitions, with a major distinctive carve-out for business combinations of entities under common control.

## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Converged with, but Not Identical to, IFRS

Ind AS 103 is India's national standard, converged with IFRS 3 for most acquisitions, but containing a MAJOR, distinctive carve-out for business combinations of entities UNDER COMMON CONTROL – an area IFRS 3 does NOT address at all. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
