# Accounting Standard for Treasury Shares and Appropriation of Legal Reserve

Accounting Standard for Treasury Shares and Appropriation of Legal Reserve is part of the Japan GAAP framework.

This standard is part of the Japan GAAP framework issued by the Accounting Standards Board of Japan (ASBJ). It provides guidance on accounting standard for treasury shares and appropriation of legal reserve.
