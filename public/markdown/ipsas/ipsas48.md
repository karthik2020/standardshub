# Transfer Expenses

Establishes principles for transfer providers accounting for transfer expenses with and without binding arrangements, covering transfer right assets, transfer obligation liabilities, and variable consideration provisions. Effective 1 January 2026.

IPSAS 48 Transfer Expenses prescribes financial reporting requirements for transfer providers providing goods, services, or other assets to another entity without directly receiving anything in return. It establishes a mirror-image framework to IPSAS 47, addressing transfer expenses without binding arrangements (provisions or cease-to-control expense) and with binding arrangements (identifying transfer rights, recognizing transfer right assets or transfer obligation liabilities, carrying-amount-based measurement, IPSAS 19-routed variable consideration, and non-performance conversions to financial assets). Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2026.
