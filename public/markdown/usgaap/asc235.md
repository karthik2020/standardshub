# Notes to Financial Statements

Governs the disclosure of significant accounting policies in the notes to financial statements, ensuring users understand the basis for reported figures.

ASC 235 requires disclosure of all significant accounting policies as an integral part of the financial statements, with particular prominence for policies involving acceptable alternatives or unusual applications of GAAP.
