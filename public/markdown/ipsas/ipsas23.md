# Revenue from Non-Exchange Transactions (Taxes and Transfers)

Prescribes the recognition and measurement of revenue from non-exchange transactions (taxes, transfers, grants, donations, fines, in-kind contributions), including the stipulation (condition vs restriction) distinction and asset-vs-outflow recognition rules. Effective 30 June 2008.

IPSAS 23 Revenue from Non-Exchange Transactions prescribes the recognition and measurement of revenue from non-exchange transactions, including taxes, grants, donations, and fines, using the stipulation and asset/outflow concepts.
