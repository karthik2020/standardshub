# Climate-Related Disclosures

The IPSASB's inaugural Sustainability Reporting Standard, providing principles for disclosing climate-related physical and transition risks, opportunities, governance, strategy, scenario analysis, and GHG emissions (Scopes 1, 2, 3) through a long-term fiscal sustainability lens.

IPSASB SRS 1 Climate-Related Disclosures establishes public sector sustainability reporting requirements aligned with IFRS S2 while incorporating key public sector adaptations. It requires disclosures across governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics and targets, focusing on climate risks and opportunities that affect an entity's long-term fiscal sustainability. Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2028.
