# Reference Rate Reform

Provides optional, temporary relief for contract modifications and hedging relationships affected by reference rate reform, most notably the LIBOR to SOFR transition.

ASC 848 provides optional expedients and exceptions for applying US GAAP to contract modifications and hedging relationships affected by reference rate reform, allowing entities to avoid reassessment purely due to benchmark rate changes.

**Issued:** 2020-05

**Latest Amendment:** 2022-12

**Effective:** 2020-12-31

## Notice

**warning:** Status Note

ASC 848's optional relief provisions applied only through their sunset date of December 31, 2024 (as extended by ASU 2022-06). As of today, this relief period has EXPIRED – ASC 848 is now substantially historical/transitional guidance. Contract modifications and hedging relationship accounting elected under ASC 848 during its effective window retain the accounting treatment applied at the time; new elections are no longer available under the sunset relief itself.
