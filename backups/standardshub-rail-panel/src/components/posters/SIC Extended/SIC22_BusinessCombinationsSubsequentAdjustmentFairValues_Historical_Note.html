SIC-22 — Business Combinations—Subsequent Adjustment of Fair Values and Goodwill Initially Reported
Historical reference note · Issued 1998 · Withdrawn 2004
⚠ WITHDRAWN — Superseded by IFRS 3 Business Combinations, effective for periods beginning on or after 31 March 2004
What SIC-22 addressed
SIC-22 addressed how to account for ADJUSTMENTS made, AFTER the period a business combination was initially accounted for, to the fair values of identifiable assets and liabilities acquired, or to the cost of the combination (and hence to GOODWILL) — for example, where the initial accounting was determined only PROVISIONALLY and later finalised, or where errors in the original figures were subsequently identified.
The conclusion it reached
SIC-22 distinguished between adjustments made within an allowed "finalisation" window (treated as adjustments to the ORIGINAL acquisition accounting, applied retrospectively to the acquisition date) versus adjustments arising from LATER events or new information (generally treated as changes in accounting estimates or error corrections under IAS 8, NOT as adjustments to goodwill).
Where this guidance lives today
Superseded byIFRS 3 Business Combinations (2004, and subsequent revisions)
Why withdrawnIFRS 3 incorporated a formal, explicitly defined "MEASUREMENT PERIOD" concept (originally up to 12 months from the acquisition date) directly into the main standard, replacing SIC-22's less precisely bounded finalisation window
Current statusThe underlying distinction — measurement-period adjustments versus later estimate changes/error corrections — remains good law today under IFRS 3's own measurement period provisions
