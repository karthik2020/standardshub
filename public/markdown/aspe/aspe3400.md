# Revenue

Revenue is part of the Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises (ASPE).

This standard is part of the ASPE framework issued by the Canadian Accounting Standards Board. It provides guidance on revenue.

**Issued:** 2006-02

**Latest Amendment:** 2016-01

**Effective:** 2006-02-01

## History & Development

History identifier: `aspe3400`

## Notice

**warning:** Revenue Recognition Retains an Older Model

ASPE Section 3400 retains the risk-and-rewards-of-ownership framework instead of adopting the 5-step control-based model used by IFRS 15, ASC 606, and revised ASBE No. 14.
