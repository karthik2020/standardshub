# Investments in Associates and Joint Ventures

Prescribes the accounting for investments in associates and joint ventures – the equity method as the core, the 20% significant-influence presumption, held-for-sale classification, discontinuance of the equity method, the deficit floor, and impairment of the net investment. Effective 1 January 2017. (IAS 28 equivalent; supersedes IPSAS 7.)

IPSAS 36 Investments in Associates and Joint Ventures prescribes how a public-sector entity accounts for investments in associates and joint ventures over which it has significant influence, requiring the equity method as the core approach. It addresses the 20% significant-influence presumption, classification as held for sale and discontinuance of the equity method, changes in ownership interest, the deficit floor where losses exceed the investment, impairment of the net investment, and transitional provisions. It supersedes IPSAS 7 (Investments in Associates).
