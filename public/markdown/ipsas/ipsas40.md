# Public Sector Combinations

Establishes principles for classifying a public sector combination as an amalgamation or an acquisition, and how a reporting entity accounts for it – amalgamations via the modified pooling-of-interests method and acquisitions via the acquisition method, including goodwill, bargain purchases, non-controlling interests and combinations under common control. Effective 1 January 2019. (IFRS 3 equivalent.)

IPSAS 40 Public Sector Combinations establishes principles for identifying and classifying a public sector combination as an amalgamation or an acquisition and for accounting for it accordingly. It prescribes the modified pooling-of-interests method for amalgamations and the acquisition method for acquisitions, covers the identification of the acquirer and the 'operation' test, the recognition and measurement of acquired assets, liabilities and deferred tax, goodwill and bargain purchases, non-controlling interests, acquisitions achieved in stages, combinations under common control, and the related disclosure and transitional requirements.
