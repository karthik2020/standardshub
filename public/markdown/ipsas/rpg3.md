# Reporting Service Performance Information

Establishes a principles-based framework for reporting service performance information in GPFRs, defining inputs, outputs, outcomes, efficiency, and effectiveness, and requiring display of objectives, indicators, and total service costs.

RPG 3 Reporting Service Performance Information guides public sector entities in reporting on their service delivery achievements, efficiency, and effectiveness. It defines the core five-part performance model (inputs, outputs, outcomes, efficiency, and effectiveness), provides criteria for selecting key quantitative and qualitative performance indicators, requires a three-way comparative display (planned, actual, and prior actual) alongside total costs, and addresses location decisions and sustainability program metrics.
