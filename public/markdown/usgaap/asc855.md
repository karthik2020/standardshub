# Subsequent Events

Addresses the accounting and disclosure requirements for events occurring after the balance sheet date but before financial statements are issued.

ASC 855 distinguishes between Type I subsequent events (recognised in financial statements because they provide additional evidence about conditions existing at the balance sheet date) and Type II subsequent events (not recognised but disclosed if material, because they arise from conditions that did not exist at the balance sheet date).

## Notice

**warning:** Key Divergence from IAS 10

ASC 855 uses a two-tier evaluation period (issued vs available to be issued) based on entity type, while IAS 10 uses a single approval date. ASC 855 also explicitly requires disclosure of the specific evaluation date, a prescriptive requirement not present in IAS 10.
