# ASC 930 – Extractive Activities–Mining

Governs exploration and evaluation costs, stripping costs, and asset retirement obligations for mining operations.

ASC 930 addresses capitalization criteria for exploration and evaluation costs, stripping costs during the production phase, and reclamation obligations. A key divergence from IFRS 6 is the absence of IFRS 6's grandfather policy for existing capitalization practices.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** Divergence from IFRS 6/IFRIC 20

ASC 930 diverges from IFRS 6 by not permitting the grandfathering of existing capitalization policies. IFRIC 20's stripping cost model also differs from ASC 930's production-phase stripping approach.
