# ASC 940 – Financial Services–Brokers and Dealers

Governs trade date accounting, customer protection through asset segregation, and fail-to-deliver positions for brokers and dealers.

ASC 940 addresses trade date versus settlement date accounting, the universal fair value through earnings requirement for trading securities, receivables and payables to customers and broker-dealers, and fails to deliver and receive. It also establishes customer protection requirements for asset segregation.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No dedicated IFRS broker-dealer standard exists. While general IFRS standards on financial instruments apply, ASC 940's specific broker-dealer guidance lacks a direct counterpart.
