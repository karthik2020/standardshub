# Financial Instruments: Recognition and Measurement

An unusual, transitioning Standard. IPSAS 41 (Financial Instruments) absorbed almost all of IPSAS 29's recognition and measurement content – paragraphs 1–79 (incl. the objective) are deleted – but paragraphs 80–126 survive as an OPTIONAL legacy hedge-accounting model: IPSAS 41 (para. 177) permits an entity to elect to continue applying IPSAS 29's hedge accounting. Effective 1 January 2013.

IPSAS 29 Financial Instruments: Recognition and Measurement is a transitional Standard. Following IPSAS 41's issuance in August 2018, paragraphs 1–79 (objective, scope, and most recognition and measurement rules) were deleted; that substantive content now resides in IPSAS 41. The surviving, authoritative portion is paragraphs 80–126 – an optional legacy hedge-accounting model that IPSAS 41 (para. 177) continues to permit; an entity may elect to apply IPSAS 29's hedge accounting instead of adopting IPSAS 41's own hedge accounting requirements. Consequently IPSAS 29 is 'mostly superseded, with a narrow legacy option remaining'; this poster covers only that surviving, optional slice.
