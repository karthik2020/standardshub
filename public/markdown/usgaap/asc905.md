# ASC 905 – Agriculture

Establishes accounting principles for agricultural activities, including measurement of agricultural produce and biological assets under US GAAP.

ASC 905 provides guidance for agricultural producers and cooperatives on inventory measurement, liability recognition, and revenue recognition. It emphasizes historical cost as the default measurement basis with a market value exception for certain agricultural products, diverging significantly from IAS 41's comprehensive fair value model for biological assets.

**Issued:** 1974-07

## Notice

**warning:** Divergence from IAS 41

ASC 905 uses historical cost as the default measurement with a market value exception, while IAS 41 requires all biological assets to be measured at fair value less costs to sell, with changes recognized in profit or loss.
