# Income Statement

Governs the presentation and format of the Income Statement, including expense classification, unusual item disclosure, and earnings per share requirements.

ASC 225 governs the format and presentation of the Income Statement, addressing single-step vs multi-step formats, unusual or infrequent item disclosure, and business interruption insurance recoveries.
