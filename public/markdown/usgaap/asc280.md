# Segment Reporting

Governs segment reporting based on the chief operating decision maker's perspective, including the 75% reporting sufficiency test and significant segment expense disclosure.

ASC 280 governs segment reporting based on the management approach, requiring disclosure of segment information as reviewed by the chief operating decision maker (CODM).
