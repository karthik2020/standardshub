# Leases (Legacy Standard)

Legacy standard governing lease accounting, superseded by ASC 842 for periods beginning after December 15, 2018.

ASC 840 was the prior US GAAP standard for lease accounting. It distinguished between capital leases and operating leases for lessees, with operating leases generally not recognised on the balance sheet. ASC 840 was superseded by ASC 842, which introduced a single lessee model requiring balance-sheet recognition for virtually all leases.
