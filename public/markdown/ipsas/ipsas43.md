# Leases

Sets out recognition, measurement, presentation, and disclosure principles for leases, covering lessee and lessor accounting, concessionary leases, sale and leaseback transactions, and transition. Effective 1 January 2025.

IPSAS 43 Leases sets out the principles for recognition, measurement, presentation, and disclosure of leases for lessees and lessors. It covers lessee recognition exemptions for short-term and low-value leases, right-of-use asset and lease liability measurement, concessionary leases, subsequent measurement and modifications, lessor classification (finance vs operating), finance lease accounting, operating leases, sale and leaseback transactions, disclosure requirements, and transitional provisions. Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025.
