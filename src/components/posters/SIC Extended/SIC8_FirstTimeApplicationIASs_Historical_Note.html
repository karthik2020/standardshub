SIC-8 — First-time Application of IASs as the Primary Basis of Accounting
Historical reference note · Issued 1998 · Withdrawn 2003
⚠ WITHDRAWN — Superseded entirely by IFRS 1 First-time Adoption of International Financial Reporting Standards (2003)
What SIC-8 addressed
SIC-8 addressed how an entity adopting International Accounting Standards (IASs) for the FIRST TIME as its primary basis of accounting — having previously reported under a different framework, such as local GAAP — should apply IASs, generally requiring full RETROSPECTIVE application as if IASs had always been applied, subject to any specific transitional provisions within individual standards.
Why it was replaced, not merely revised
Unlike most other withdrawn SICs, SIC-8 was not folded into an existing standard — it was replaced by an ENTIRELY NEW, comprehensive standard, IFRS 1, dedicated specifically to first-time adoption. IFRS 1 goes far beyond SIC-8's brief guidance, providing detailed MANDATORY and OPTIONAL exemptions from full retrospective application across many specific standards (e.g. business combinations, employee benefits, cumulative translation differences), reflecting the far larger and more complex population of entities that adopted IFRS as it expanded globally after 2003.
Where this guidance lives today
