# Accounting Changes and Error Corrections

Governs accounting for and reporting changes in accounting principle, changes in accounting estimate, changes in reporting entity, and corrections of errors in previously issued financial statements.

ASC 250 governs accounting for and reporting (a) a change in accounting principle, (b) a change in accounting estimate, (c) a change in the reporting entity, and (d) the correction of an error in previously issued financial statements.
