# Property, Plant and Equipment

Prescribes accounting for property, plant and equipment so users can discern an entity's investment in it and how that investment changes, covering recognition, measurement (historical cost and current value models), depreciation, impairment, derecognition, and disclosures including heritage assets. Effective 1 January 2025.

IPSAS 45 Property, Plant and Equipment prescribes the accounting treatment for property, plant and equipment so that users of financial statements can discern an entity's investment in it and how that investment changes. It covers recognition criteria, initial measurement (elements of cost, non-exchange acquisitions, exchanges), subsequent measurement (historical cost model and current value model with current operational value or fair value), depreciation (component approach), finite and indefinite useful lives, impairment, derecognition, heritage assets, infrastructure assets, weapons systems, disclosure requirements, and transitional provisions. Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025.
