# ASC 974 – Real Estate–Real Estate Investment Trusts

Addresses organizational costs, property acquisition costs, and non-GAAP Funds From Operations for real estate investment trusts.

ASC 974 clarifies that REIT status is fundamentally a tax concept rather than a distinct GAAP measurement model. It covers organizational and offering costs, property acquisition costs, and Funds From Operations as a non-GAAP supplemental metric reconciled from net income. No dedicated IFRS REIT standard exists.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No dedicated IFRS REIT standard exists. REIT qualification is fundamentally a tax concept in US GAAP, and no equivalent tax-driven structure exists under IFRS.
