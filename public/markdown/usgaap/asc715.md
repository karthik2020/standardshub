# Compensation–Retirement Benefits

Governs defined benefit and defined contribution pension plans, other postretirement benefits, and pension expense measurement.

ASC 715 governs retirement benefits including defined benefit pension plans, defined contribution plans, and other postretirement benefits, establishing recognition, measurement, and disclosure requirements for plan assets, obligations, and expenses.
