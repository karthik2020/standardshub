# General Standards of Financial Statement Presentation

General Standards of Financial Statement Presentation is part of the Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises (ASPE).

This standard is part of the ASPE framework issued by the Canadian Accounting Standards Board. It provides guidance on general standards of financial statement presentation.
