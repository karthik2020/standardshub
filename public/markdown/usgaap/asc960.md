# ASC 960 – Plan Accounting–Defined Benefit Pension Plans

Addresses plan-level defined benefit pension plan reporting including statements of net assets available for benefits and actuarial present value disclosures.

ASC 960 governs plan-level reporting for defined benefit pension plans, covering the statement of net assets available for benefits, the statement of changes in net assets, the actuarial present value of accumulated plan benefits, and the contrast with ASC 715's projected benefit obligation. It serves as the counterpart to IAS 26.

## Notice

**info:** Converged with IAS 26

ASC 960 aligns closely with IAS 26 for defined benefit plan reporting. Both standards require statements of net assets available for benefits and changes in net assets, though measurement of obligations may differ.
