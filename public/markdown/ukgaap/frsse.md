# FRSSE – Financial Reporting Standard for Smaller Entities (Withdrawn) | Extended Reference

The FRSSE was the pre-2015 simplified accounting regime for small companies, withdrawn for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2016 and superseded by FRS 102 Section 1A and FRS 105.

The FRSSE provided a self-contained set of simplified recognition, measurement, and disclosure requirements for entities meeting the small company size thresholds, allowing them to avoid applying the full patchwork of old UK GAAP standards. Its withdrawal was part of the 2015 wholesale overhaul of UK GAAP, replacing it with a more granular two-tier simplification structure within the new FRS 100–105 suite. Unlike today's IFRS-anchored standards, the FRSSE was developed largely independently of IFRS, reflecting a pre-2015 insular UK-specific approach.

## Notice

**warning:** Superseded by FRS 105

The FRSSE was withdrawn for accounting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2016, replaced by FRS 102 Section 1A for small companies and FRS 105 for micro-entities. Unlike today's explicitly IFRS-anchored UK GAAP suite, the FRSSE was developed largely independently of IFRS, reflecting a pre-2015 insular UK-specific approach.
