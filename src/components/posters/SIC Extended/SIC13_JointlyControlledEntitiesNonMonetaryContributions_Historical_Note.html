SIC-13 – Jointly Controlled Entities–Non-Monetary Contributions by Venturers
Historical reference note · Issued 1998 · Withdrawn 2013
⚠ WITHDRAWN – Superseded by IFRS 11 Joint Arrangements, effective for periods beginning 1 January 2013
What SIC-13 addressed
SIC-13 addressed how a "venturer" should account for a GAIN OR LOSS on contributing a NON-MONETARY ASSET to a jointly controlled entity (JCE) in exchange for an equity interest in that entity – a common structuring question when two or more parties pooled assets to form a joint venture vehicle.
The conclusion it reached
The venturer should recognise the portion of the gain or loss attributable to the OTHER venturers' equity interests (i.e. NOT the portion attributable to its own retained interest), provided the significant risks and rewards of ownership of the contributed asset had genuinely been transferred to the jointly controlled entity, and the gain or loss could be measured reliably.
Where this guidance lives today
Superseded byIFRS 11 Joint Arrangements (2011)
Why withdrawnIFRS 11 replaced the "jointly controlled entity" concept with a two-way classification of joint arrangements as either "joint operations" or "joint ventures", restructuring how contributions and interests in such arrangements are accounted for
Current statusContributions of non-monetary assets to a joint venture are now addressed through general IFRS derecognition and equity-method principles (IAS 28, IFRS 10, IAS 16) rather than a standalone SIC
