# Financial Instruments: Presentation

Establishes principles for presenting financial instruments – classifying and measuring a financial instrument as a liability or as net assets/equity, accounting for puttable instruments and pro rata liquidation-only instruments, the accounting for compound financial instruments and treasury shares, and the offsetting of a financial asset with a financial liability. Effective 1 January 2013. (IAS 32 equivalent, adapted for the public sector.) Complements IPSAS 41 (recognition/measurement) and IPSAS 30 (disclosure).

IPSAS 28 Financial Instruments: Presentation establishes principles for presenting financial instruments in the financial statements of public sector entities. It sets out the criteria for classifying and measuring a financial instrument as a liability or as net assets/equity, and the accounting for puttable instruments, pro rata liquidation-only instruments, compound financial instruments, treasury shares, and the offsetting of a financial asset with a financial liability. It complements IPSAS 41 (recognition and measurement) and IPSAS 30 (disclosure).
