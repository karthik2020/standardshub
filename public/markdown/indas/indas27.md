## Notice

**warning:** Ind AS – Converged with IAS 27, with a Genuine Mandatory Filing Divergence

Ind AS 27's measurement options for investments in subsidiaries/JVs/associates mirror IAS 27 closely. The distinctive Indian feature is that the Companies Act, 2013 MANDATES every Indian company to prepare and file STANDALONE (separate) financial statements, REGARDLESS of whether it also prepares consolidated financial statements – unlike many IFRS jurisdictions where separate FS preparation may not be a legal requirement. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
