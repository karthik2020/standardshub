# FRS 102 – The Financial Reporting Standard Applicable in the UK and Republic of Ireland | Extended Reference

FRS 102 is the core standard of the new UK GAAP suite, based on the IFRS for SMEs Standard, used by the vast majority of UK and Irish companies, LLPs, and other entities.

FRS 102 applies to entities not required or electing to use IFRS or FRS 101, and not applying the FRS 105 micro-entities regime. It uses the IFRS for SMEs recognition and measurement principles but retains some UK-specific policy options, such as elective capitalisation of development costs and borrowing costs, a rebuttable 10-year presumption for goodwill amortisation, and a simplified two-tier financial instruments model. Unlike full IFRS, which mandates these treatments, FRS 102 offers proportionate flexibility for entities without public accountability.

## Notice

**info:** FRS 102 vs Full IFRS

FRS 102 is based on the IFRS for SMEs Standard but introduces several material simplifications and policy choices not available under full IFRS. Key divergences include elective development cost and borrowing cost treatments, mandatory goodwill amortisation, and a simplified financial instruments model, reflecting its design for entities without public accountability.
