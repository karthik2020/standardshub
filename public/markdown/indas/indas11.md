## Notice

**danger:** Withdrawn Standard – Superseded by Ind AS 115

Ind AS 11 was withdrawn effective the same transition date as IAS 11's global withdrawal, replaced by Ind AS 115's five-step revenue recognition model. This page is retained for HISTORICAL reference and to help practitioners understand the transition impact on construction/EPC-sector companies. Section 11 covers the full comparison with its IFRS counterpart.
