Conceptual Framework Linkage

A deliberately simpler classification and impairment model
ASPE Section 3856 governs the RECOGNITION, MEASUREMENT, and PRESENTATION of FINANCIAL instruments – FINANCIAL assets, FINANCIAL liabilities, and EQUITY instruments. GENUINELY distinctively, ASPE applies a MUCH simpler, TWO-category CLASSIFICATION model (Section 3) and an OLDER, INCURRED-loss impairment APPROACH (Section 7) – RATHER than IFRS 9'S three-CATEGORY, business-MODEL-driven classification AND its MANDATORY expected CREDIT loss (ECL) model.
2

Scope

Financial assets, financial liabilities, and equity instruments
This Section applies TO all FINANCIAL instruments, WORKING alongside SECTION 3051 (INVESTMENTS), Section 3210 (LONG-term debt), and SECTION 3251 (EQUITY) for THEIR own MORE specific PRESENTATION guidance.
3

THE CLASSIFICATION MODEL – Two Categories, Not Three

A significantly simpler structure than IFRS 9
CategoryDescription
Cost / amortized costMOST receivables, PAYABLES, and SIMPLE debt INSTRUMENTS – the DEFAULT category
Fair value through net incomeINSTRUMENTS QUOTED in an ACTIVE market (MANDATORY), or OTHER instruments WHERE the FAIR value OPTION is ELECTED
THIS two-CATEGORY structure is SIGNIFICANTLY simpler THAN IFRS 9'S own THREE-category model (AMORTIZED cost, FAIR value through OCI, and FAIR value through PROFIT or LOSS), WHICH depends ON a detailed BUSINESS-model and CONTRACTUAL cash-FLOW-characteristics assessment – ASPE AVOIDS this COMPLEXITY entirely.
4

Basic Financial Instruments – Cost or Amortized Cost

The default category for most everyday instruments
MOST basic FINANCIAL instruments – ACCOUNTS receivable, ACCOUNTS payable, and CONVENTIONAL fixed-OR floating-rate DEBT – are MEASURED at COST or AMORTIZED cost, USING the EFFECTIVE interest METHOD where APPLICABLE (see the COMPANION Section 3210 poster).
5

Quoted Instruments – Mandatory Fair Value Through Net Income

No OCI available, so changes flow directly to the bottom line
FINANCIAL instruments QUOTED in an ACTIVE market are MEASURED at FAIR value, WITH changes RECOGNIZED directly WITHIN net INCOME – GIVEN ASPE'S absence OF any OCI MECHANISM (see the COMPANION Section 1520 poster), THERE is no ALTERNATIVE "fair VALUE through OCI" category THE way IFRS 9 offers FOR certain debt AND equity instruments.
6

The Elective Fair Value Option

Available where it reduces measurement inconsistency
AN enterprise MAY elect the FAIR value OPTION for OTHER financial INSTRUMENTS (BEYOND those QUOTED in an ACTIVE market) WHERE doing SO eliminates OR significantly REDUCES a measurement OR recognition INCONSISTENCY that WOULD otherwise ARISE from MEASURING related ASSETS and LIABILITIES on DIFFERENT bases.
7

THE IMPAIRMENT MODEL – Incurred Loss, Not Expected Credit Loss

A genuinely significant divergence from IFRS 9 and even from ASBE
FOR financial ASSETS measured AT cost OR amortized COST, an IMPAIRMENT loss is RECOGNIZED only WHEN there IS an INDICATION of impairment – TO the EXTENT the CARRYING amount EXCEEDS the HIGHEST of: the PRESENT value OF expected FUTURE cash FLOWS; the AMOUNT that COULD be REALIZED by SELLING the asset; OR the AMOUNT expected TO be REALIZED by EXERCISING rights TO any COLLATERAL. THIS is a GENUINE "INCURRED loss" model – REQUIRING objective EVIDENCE that a LOSS event HAS already OCCURRED, RATHER than IFRS 9'S mandatory EXPECTED credit LOSS (ECL) model (ADOPTED internationally in 2018), WHICH requires FORWARD-looking loss PROVISIONING even BEFORE a SPECIFIC loss EVENT has OCCURRED. NOTABLY, THIS also DIFFERS from ASBE'S own 2018/2021 ADOPTION of an ECL-style MODEL (see the COMPANION Chinese GAAP poster) – ASPE RETAINS the OLDER, incurred-LOSS approach that WAS common INTERNATIONALLY prior TO the post-financial-CRISIS shift TOWARD expected-LOSS provisioning.
8

Embedded Derivatives – a Genuine Non-Bifurcation Choice

A practical simplification for certain host contracts
ASPE provides an ACCOUNTING policy CHOICE permitting an ENTERPRISE, in CERTAIN circumstances, NOT to SEPARATELY identify and FAIR-value embedded DERIVATIVES within CERTAIN host CONTRACTS – a GENUINELY practical SIMPLIFICATION relative TO the MORE detailed embedded-DERIVATIVE bifurcation RULES that HAVE historically APPLIED, and CONTINUE to apply IN narrower circumstances, INTERNATIONALLY.
9

Basic Hedge Accounting

Simplified rules for common hedging relationships
ASPE permits BASIC hedge ACCOUNTING for SPECIFIC, common HEDGING relationships (e.g. AN interest RATE swap CONVERTING floating-RATE debt to FIXED-rate, or a FORWARD exchange CONTRACT hedging a FOREIGN currency COMMITMENT), WITH substantially SIMPLIFIED rules RELATIVE to IFRS 9'S own MORE complex hedge-ACCOUNTING model.
10

Cross-Reference – Retractable or Mandatorily Redeemable Shares

The liability classification underlying Section 3251's own relief
THIS Section SUPPLIES the UNDERLYING financial-INSTRUMENT classification PRINCIPLES requiring RETRACTABLE or MANDATORILY redeemable SHARES to BE presented AS a financial LIABILITY – WITH Section 3251'S own SPECIFIC relief (CHARGING the RESULTING adjustment TO retained EARNINGS, rather THAN net income) BUILT on TOP of this Section'S general CLASSIFICATION framework (see the COMPANION Section 3251 poster).

Worked Example – the Incurred Loss Impairment Test

Requiring an actual loss event, not merely a forward-looking risk assessment
Example: A customer's deteriorating financial position
A company holds a $400,000 receivable from a customer measured at cost. The customer files for bankruptcy protection during the year – an objective indication of impairment. The present value of expected future cash flows from the receivable is estimated at $220,000.
GIVEN the BANKRUPTCY filing IS an OBJECTIVE, ALREADY-occurred LOSS event, the COMPANY recognizes an IMPAIRMENT loss OF $400,000 − $220,000 = $180,000 – CONSISTENT with the INCURRED-loss model'S REQUIREMENT for OBJECTIVE evidence OF impairment, RATHER than a MERELY forward-LOOKING expected-LOSS assessment.
12

Worked Example – Classifying a Quoted vs Unquoted Investment

The simple, binary classification test in action
Example: Two portfolio investments with different classification outcomes
A company holds a small stake in a publicly traded company (quoted, active market) and a small stake in an unquoted private company. Neither investment provides significant influence.
The PUBLICLY traded STAKE is MANDATORILY measured AT fair VALUE through NET income; the UNQUOTED stake is MEASURED at COST (unless the COMPANY elects the FAIR value option) – a SIMPLE, binary CLASSIFICATION test, RATHER than IFRS 9'S more ELABORATE business-MODEL analysis.

Quick-Reference Glossary

Incurred loss model
An impairment approach recognizing a loss only where objective evidence indicates a loss event has already occurred, rather than forward-looking expected loss provisioning.
Basic financial instrument
A conventional receivable, payable, or debt instrument measured at cost or amortized cost, the default category under Section 3856.
