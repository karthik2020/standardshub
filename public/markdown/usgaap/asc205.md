# Presentation of Financial Statements

Governs the overall presentation of financial statements, including complete set requirements, going concern assessment, discontinued operations, and liquidation basis of accounting.

ASC 205 supplies the overall requirements for a complete set of financial statements, plus specialised guidance on going concern, discontinued operations, and liquidation basis of accounting.
