# Reorganizations

Addresses accounting for reorganizations under Chapter 11 of the US Bankruptcy Code, including fresh-start reporting when specific conditions are met.

ASC 852 provides guidance for entities undergoing reorganization under US bankruptcy law. When an entity meets the fresh-start eligibility test – demonstrating insolvency and a significant change in ownership – the emerging entity restates its assets and liabilities to fair value and resets its accumulated deficit, effectively presenting as a new reporting entity.

## Notice

**warning:** US-Specific Standard – No Direct IFRS Equivalent

ASC 852 addresses Chapter 11 bankruptcy reorganizations, a US-specific legal framework. IFRS does not have an equivalent standard; entities emerging from bankruptcy under IFRS typically apply the going-concern assessment under IAS 1 and business combination accounting under IFRS 3 where applicable.
