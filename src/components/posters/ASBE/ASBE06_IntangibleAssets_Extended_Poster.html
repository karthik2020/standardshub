Conceptual Framework Linkage

Identifiable non-monetary assets without physical substance
ASBE No. 6 governs IDENTIFIABLE non-monetary ASSETS WITHOUT physical SUBSTANCE — patents, TRADEMARKS, copyrights, and (GENUINELY distinctively) LAND USE RIGHTS held for the enterprise's OWN operational USE. The Standard is BROADLY converged with IAS 38 internationally, INCLUDING the SAME research/development CAPITALISATION split, though it SHARES the SAME cost-model-ONLY restriction seen ELSEWHERE across the ASBE ASSET standards.
2

Scope and Recognition Criteria

Identifiability, control, and probable future economic benefit
An INTANGIBLE asset is RECOGNISED where it is IDENTIFIABLE (SEPARABLE, or ARISING from CONTRACTUAL/legal RIGHTS), CONTROLLED by the ENTERPRISE, and EXPECTED to GENERATE probable FUTURE economic BENEFITS, WITH its cost MEASURABLE reliably.
3

Land Use Rights as Intangible Assets

A genuinely distinctive classification reflecting China's land system
GIVEN China's STATE (or COLLECTIVE) land OWNERSHIP system, enterprises HOLD "LAND USE RIGHTS" — a CONTRACTUAL right to USE land for a DEFINED period — RATHER than FREEHOLD land OWNERSHIP. WHERE such RIGHTS are held for the enterprise's OWN operational USE (as OPPOSED to leasing OUT or capital APPRECIATION, which would FALL under ASBE No. 3, Investment Property), they are CLASSIFIED as INTANGIBLE assets under THIS Standard — a GENUINELY distinctive CLASSIFICATION without a DIRECT IFRS/US GAAP PARALLEL, since NEITHER framework has an EQUIVALENT land-TENURE construct of the SAME type.
4

Initial Measurement — At Cost

Purchase price plus directly attributable costs
An INTANGIBLE asset ACQUIRED separately is MEASURED at COST — the PURCHASE price PLUS directly ATTRIBUTABLE costs of PREPARING the asset for its INTENDED use. INTANGIBLE assets ACQUIRED WITHIN a business COMBINATION are measured at FAIR VALUE at the ACQUISITION date (UNLESS the combination is UNDER common CONTROL — see the COMPANION ASBE No. 2 poster).
5

Internally Generated Intangibles — the Research/Development Split

Research expensed, development capitalised if criteria are met
EXPENDITURE incurred DURING the RESEARCH phase (ORIGINAL, planned INVESTIGATION undertaken with the PROSPECT of gaining NEW scientific or TECHNICAL knowledge) is EXPENSED as INCURRED — the enterprise CANNOT yet DEMONSTRATE that a FUTURE economic BENEFIT will FLOW. EXPENDITURE incurred DURING the DEVELOPMENT phase (APPLYING research FINDINGS to a PLAN for producing NEW or SUBSTANTIALLY improved PRODUCTS or processes) MAY be CAPITALISED, but ONLY if SPECIFIC criteria are MET (Section 6) — a SPLIT CONVERGED with IAS 38's OWN research/development DISTINCTION internationally.

Subsequent Measurement — Amortisation and the No-Revaluation Rule

Cost model only, consistent with fixed assets
CONSISTENT with the SAME cost-model-ONLY restriction seen for FIXED assets (ASBE No. 4), NO revaluation MODEL is available for INTANGIBLE assets under ASBE No. 6 — they are CARRIED at COST less ACCUMULATED amortisation (for FINITE-life intangibles) and IMPAIRMENT.
8

Finite vs Indefinite Useful Life

Amortisation only for finite-life intangibles
Intangible ASSETS with a FINITE useful LIFE are AMORTISED systematically OVER that LIFE. Intangible ASSETS with an INDEFINITE useful LIFE (WHERE there is NO foreseeable LIMIT to the PERIOD over WHICH the asset is EXPECTED to GENERATE benefits) are NOT amortised, but are TESTED for IMPAIRMENT ANNUALLY — CONSISTENT with IAS 38's OWN approach internationally.
9

Worked Example — Capitalising Development Costs

Splitting research and development expenditure
Example: A new product development project
A company spends RMB 3,000,000 on early-stage research into a new technology (technical feasibility not yet established), and a further RMB 5,000,000 on subsequent development after feasibility, intent, and resources are all clearly demonstrated.
The RMB 3,000,000 RESEARCH cost is EXPENSED as incurred. The RMB 5,000,000 DEVELOPMENT cost is CAPITALISED as an intangible asset, since ALL the development-phase CRITERIA (Section 6) are DEMONSTRATED — to be AMORTISED over its USEFUL life once the asset is READY for its intended USE.
10

Worked Example — Amortising a Land Use Right

A distinctively Chinese intangible asset
Example: A 50-year land use right for a factory site
A company acquires a land use right for its own factory site, at a cost of RMB 40,000,000, for a defined term of 50 years.
The company recognises an INTANGIBLE ASSET of RMB 40,000,000, AMORTISED on a straight-line BASIS over the 50-year TERM: RMB 40,000,000 ÷ 50 = RMB 800,000 per year — a GENUINELY distinctive CLASSIFICATION reflecting China's land TENURE system.

Quick-Reference Glossary

Land use right (土地使用权)
A contractual right to use state- or collectively-owned land for a defined term, classified as an intangible asset when held for the enterprise's own operational use.
Development phase
The stage of an internal project applying research findings to a plan for producing new or improved products, where expenditure may be capitalised if strict criteria are met.
