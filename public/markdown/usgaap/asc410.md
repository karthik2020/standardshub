# Asset Retirement and Environmental Obligations

Governs asset retirement obligations and environmental remediation liabilities, including initial recognition at fair value and subsequent accretion expense.

ASC 410 governs asset retirement obligations and environmental obligations, including initial recognition at fair value, accretion of the related asset retirement cost, and changes in estimated cash flows.
