# ASC 926 – Entertainment–Films

Establishes accounting for film costs, participations, residuals, and revenue recognition based on the ultimate revenue estimation method.

ASC 926 covers capitalization of film costs, the individual-film-forecast method for amortization based on ultimate revenue estimates, participations and residuals, and impairment of film costs. It contrasts with IAS 38 and IAS 2 in several key areas.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** Divergence from IAS 38/IAS 2

ASC 926's ultimate revenue estimation method and film cost capitalization diverge from IAS 38's intangible asset model. Differences also exist in the treatment of participations and residuals compared to IAS 2 inventory norms.
