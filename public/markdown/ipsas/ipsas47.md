# Revenue

Establishes a single, unified revenue framework replacing IPSAS 9 and IPSAS 23, distinguishing transactions with and without binding arrangements and introducing compliance obligations, capital transfers, and variable consideration rules. Effective 1 January 2026.

IPSAS 47 Revenue establishes principles for recognizing and measuring revenue from both exchange and non-exchange transactions within a single, cohesive standard, replacing IPSAS 9 and IPSAS 23. It introduces a gateway decision based on whether a binding arrangement exists, provides accounting models for transactions without binding arrangements (including taxes and transfers) and with binding arrangements (five-criteria gate, compliance obligations satisfied over time or at a point in time, variable consideration, and allocation based on stand-alone value), and addresses capital transfers, services in-kind, and compelled transactions. Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2026.
