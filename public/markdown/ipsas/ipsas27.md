# Agriculture

Prescribes the accounting for biological assets (except bearer plants) and agricultural produce, with both measured at fair value less estimated point-of-sale costs; produce at the point of harvest is measured at fair value less costs of disposal and recognised in profit or loss. Covers recognition, measurement, gains and losses, the inability to measure fair value reliably, the biological-asset reconciliation, and related disclosures. Effective 1 April 2011. (IAS 41 equivalent.)

IPSAS 27 Agriculture prescribes the accounting treatment for biological assets (except bearer plants) and agricultural produce. Both biological assets and agricultural produce at the point of harvest are measured at fair value less estimated point-of-sale costs, with changes in fair value recognised in profit or loss. The Standard covers recognition and derecognition, measurement and its exception where fair value cannot be measured reliably, gains and losses, the reconciliation of the carrying amount of biological assets, and the related disclosures.
