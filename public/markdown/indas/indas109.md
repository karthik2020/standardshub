## Notice

**success:** Substantially converged with IAS/IFRS

Ind AS 109 mirrors IFRS 9's classification, ECL, and hedge accounting model almost exactly. The genuinely important Indian context is RBI's separate, parallel prudential provisioning framework for banks, creating a distinctive dual system still in transition.
