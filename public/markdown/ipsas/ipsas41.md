# Financial Instruments

Establishes principles for financial reporting of financial assets and financial liabilities, covering recognition, derecognition, classification, measurement, impairment, hedge accounting, and presentation. Replaces almost all of IPSAS 29. Effective 1 January 2023.

IPSAS 41 Financial Instruments establishes principles for financial reporting of financial assets and financial liabilities that present relevant, useful information for assessing the amount, timing, and uncertainty of an entity's future cash flows. It covers recognition and initial measurement, derecognition of financial assets and liabilities, classification of financial assets and liabilities, subsequent measurement including amortized cost and the effective interest method, the expected credit loss impairment model, hedge accounting, and presentation requirements. Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2023.
