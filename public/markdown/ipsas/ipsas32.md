# Service Concession Arrangements: Grantor

Prescribes the accounting by a public-sector grantor for service concession arrangements – how a grantor recognises and measures a service concession asset, the corresponding liability, and the two presentation models (financial-liability model and grantor-right model). Effective 1 January 2014. (No direct IAS/IFRS equivalent – IFRIC 12 underpins the economics.)

IPSAS 32 Service Concession Arrangements: Grantor prescribes how a public-sector entity (the grantor) accounts for a service concession arrangement in which it transfers the right to use an asset (or to construct, manage, operate or maintain it) in return for a share of the output or other considerations. It requires recognition of a service concession asset when control transfers, measurement of that asset and the corresponding liability, and application of either the financial-liability model or the grantor-right model depending on the arrangement's structure.
