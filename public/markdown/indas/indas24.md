## Notice

**warning:** Ind AS – Converged with IAS 24, with a Genuine KMP Definitional Alignment

Ind AS 24's related party identification principles mirror IAS 24 closely. The distinctive Indian feature is that 'Key Managerial Personnel' is interpreted CONSISTENTLY with the Companies Act, 2013's own STATUTORY definition (Section 2(51)) – a more PRESCRIPTIVE list of specific roles than IAS 24's own broader, principles-based 'key management personnel' concept. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
