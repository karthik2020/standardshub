# Separate Financial Statements

Prescribes the accounting and disclosure for investments in associates and joint ventures when an entity elects to present separate financial statements that include such interests but not the entities it controls. Effective 1 January 2017 – and it WITHDREW IPSAS 6 (Consolidated and Separate Financial Statements), taking over the separate-statements carve-out. (IAS 27 equivalent.)

IPSAS 34 Separate Financial Statements prescribes the accounting and disclosure for investments in associates, joint ventures and other Controlling Interests when an entity elects (or is required) to present separate financial statements that include such interests without consolidating the entities it controls. It also formalised the withdrawal of IPSAS 6 (Consolidated and Separate Financial Statements), assuming responsibility for the separate-statements measurement and disclosure carve-out, effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2017.
