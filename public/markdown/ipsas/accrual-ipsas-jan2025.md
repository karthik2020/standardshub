# Summary of Accrual IPSAS Standards Issued as of January 2025

Master index and appendix from Volume III of the 2025 IPSASB Handbook, summarizing all 43 effective accrual standards (Table A), 4 issued but not-yet-effective standards (Table B), and 7 withdrawn standards (Table C).

Accrual IPSAS Standards Issued as at January 31, 2025 provides a master index of the IPSASB accrual pronouncements suite. It organizes standards into Table A (currently effective standards IPSAS 1–46), Table B (issued but not yet effective standards IPSAS 47–50 with consequential amendment footprints), and Table C (withdrawn standards IPSAS 6, 7, 8, 13, 15, 17, 25), cross-referencing each standard across the Handbook.
