# Receivables

Governs receivables recognition, measurement, and disclosure, including nonrefundable fees, purchased credit-deteriorated assets, and troubled debt restructurings.

ASC 310 governs receivables, including nonrefundable fees and direct loan origination costs, purchased financial assets with credit deterioration, and disclosures for loan modifications to borrowers experiencing financial difficulty.
