# Consolidated and Separate Financial Statements

Withdrawn standard. IPSAS 6 Consolidated and Separate Financial Statements was withdrawn as part of the IPSASB's 2015 Consolidation Suite project, mirroring the IASB's 2011 replacement of IAS 27 with IFRS 10 and IAS 27 (2011). It was superseded for annual financial statements covering periods beginning on or after 1 January 2017 by IPSAS 35 Consolidated Financial Statements and IPSAS 34 Separate Financial Statements. Retained as a numbering placeholder with a redirect map.

IPSAS 6 Consolidated and Separate Financial Statements established the requirements for a controlling entity to present consolidated financial statements and for separate financial statements. Withdrawn as part of the IPSASB 2015 Consolidation Suite, it was superseded for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2017 by IPSAS 35 Consolidated Financial Statements and IPSAS 34 Separate Financial Statements.
