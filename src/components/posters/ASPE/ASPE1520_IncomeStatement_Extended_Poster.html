1

Conceptual Framework Linkage

A statement that ends at net income, and nowhere further
ASPE Section 1520 SETS OUT the MINIMUM content and PRESENTATION requirements FOR the INCOME Statement – the STATEMENT reporting an ENTERPRISE'S revenues, EXPENSES, and RESULTING net INCOME for a PERIOD. THE single MOST defining FEATURE of THIS Section, relative TO IFRS or US GAAP, is WHAT it does NOT include – THERE is NO Other Comprehensive INCOME concept ANYWHERE within ASPE (Section 7).
2

Scope – the Statement of Financial Performance

One of the four required primary statements
This Section applies to the PRESENTATION of the INCOME Statement – ONE of the FOUR required PRIMARY statements COMPRISING a COMPLETE set of ASPE financial STATEMENTS (ALONGSIDE the BALANCE Sheet, the STATEMENT of RETAINED Earnings or CHANGES in EQUITY, and the CASH Flow Statement).
3

Minimum Content

Revenue through net income, with discontinued operations shown separately where relevant
Line itemDescription
RevenueREVENUE recognized FOR the period, PER Section 3400
ExpensesEXPENSES incurred, PRESENTED by NATURE or FUNCTION
Income from continuing operationsThe RESULT of ONGOING business ACTIVITIES
Discontinued operationsRESULTS of OPERATIONS meeting the DISCONTINUED operations CRITERIA under SECTION 3475, PRESENTED separately
Net incomeThe FINAL, BOTTOM-line result FOR the period
4

Presentation by Nature or by Function – a Genuine Choice

No mandated single expense presentation format
ASPE does NOT mandate a SINGLE expense PRESENTATION format – an ENTERPRISE may PRESENT expenses BY their NATURE (e.g. SALARIES, depreciation, RAW materials) OR by FUNCTION (e.g. COST of goods SOLD, selling EXPENSES, administrative EXPENSES), WHICHEVER presentation PROVIDES information THAT is RELIABLE and MORE relevant.
5

Income From Continuing Operations

The ordinary, ongoing results of the business
INCOME from CONTINUING operations REFLECTS the RESULTS of the ENTERPRISE'S ONGOING business ACTIVITIES – PRESENTED separately FROM any DISCONTINUED operations, ALLOWING readers to ASSESS the UNDERLYING, ONGOING earning POWER of the BUSINESS distinct FROM operations BEING wound DOWN or SOLD.
6

Discontinued Operations

Presented separately, per Section 3475
WHERE a COMPONENT of an ENTERPRISE meets the DEFINITION of a DISCONTINUED operation UNDER Section 3475 (DISPOSAL of long-LIVED assets and DISCONTINUED operations), the RESULTS of THAT component are PRESENTED SEPARATELY from CONTINUING operations WITHIN the INCOME Statement.
7

THE DEFINING FEATURE – No Other Comprehensive Income

A structural absence that ripples throughout ASPE
UNLIKE IFRS, US GAAP, OR ASBE – ALL of WHICH include an "OTHER comprehensive INCOME" concept CAPTURING certain GAINS and LOSSES OUTSIDE of net INCOME (e.g. FOREIGN currency TRANSLATION differences, CERTAIN fair VALUE remeasurements) – ASPE has NO equivalent CONCEPT whatsoever. THERE is NO "Statement of COMPREHENSIVE Income," NO OCI reserve WITHIN equity, and NO items EVER "recycled" FROM OCI into PROFIT or loss, SIMPLY because THERE is NO OCI to BEGIN with. EVERY gain OR loss an ASPE preparer RECOGNIZES flows DIRECTLY through NET income – a GENUINELY fundamental STRUCTURAL simplification, CONSISTENT with ASPE'S broader PREFERENCE for HISTORICAL cost measurement OVER fair-VALUE-driven volatility.
8

Net Income as the Final Line

The single bottom-line result
The INCOME Statement CONCLUDES simply at "NET income" – THIS single FIGURE then FLOWS directly INTO the STATEMENT of RETAINED Earnings (or STATEMENT of CHANGES in EQUITY), COMPLETING the LINK between the TWO statements WITHOUT any INTERMEDIATE comprehensive-INCOME concept.
9

Worked Example – a Function-of-Expense Presentation

Organizing expenses by their operational role
Example: A retail company's Income Statement
A private retail company presents its Income Statement with revenue of $12,000,000, cost of goods sold of $7,200,000, selling expenses of $2,100,000, and administrative expenses of $1,400,000.
The COMPANY presents INCOME from CONTINUING operations of $12,000,000 − $7,200,000 − $2,100,000 − $1,400,000 = $1,300,000, using a FUNCTION-of-expense PRESENTATION, CONCLUDING at NET income WITH no FURTHER comprehensive-INCOME layer.
10

Worked Example – Separating Discontinued Operations

Isolating results from a wound-down business segment
Example: Winding down an unprofitable division
The same company sold an unprofitable division during the year, meeting the discontinued operations criteria, generating a $300,000 loss from that division's operations plus disposal costs.
The COMPANY presents INCOME from CONTINUING operations of $1,300,000, SEPARATELY presents a $300,000 LOSS from DISCONTINUED operations, ARRIVING at NET income of $1,300,000 − $300,000 = $1,000,000 – CLEARLY distinguishing the ONGOING business'S performance FROM the DISCONTINUED division.
