# Tangible Natural Resources Held for Conservation

Sets principles for reporting naturally occurring tangible assets managed to prevent degradation, featuring a rebuttable presumption of indefinite useful life, deemed cost via current operational value, separate SOFP presentation, and rare/endangered species disclosure exemptions. Effective 1 January 2028.

IPSAS 51 Tangible Natural Resources Held for Conservation prescribes the accounting treatment, presentation, and disclosures for naturally occurring tangible assets held primarily to prevent their degradation. It establishes recognition criteria based on service potential, initial measurement at deemed cost (current operational value per IPSAS 46) or cost, subsequent measurement under historical cost or current value models, a distinctive rebuttable presumption of indefinite useful life, impairment testing under IPSAS 21, separate line item presentation on the statement of financial position (amended IPSAS 1 para. 88(ba)), and special disclosure carve-outs for rare and endangered resources. Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2028.
