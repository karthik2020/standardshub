# ASC 978 – Real Estate–Time-Sharing Activities

Governs timeshare interval sales, relative sales value inventory costing, and uncollectible accounts under ASC 606.

ASC 978 addresses timeshare interval sales revenue recognition under ASC 606, inventory costing using the relative sales value method, and uncollectible accounts applying the variable consideration constraint. It is broadly converged with IFRS post-IFRS 15 on timeshare revenue recognition.
