# Subsidiaries

Subsidiaries is part of the Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises (ASPE).

This standard is part of the ASPE framework issued by the Canadian Accounting Standards Board. It provides guidance on subsidiaries.

**Issued:** 2006-02

**Latest Amendment:** 2016-01

**Effective:** 2006-02-01

## History & Development

History identifier: `aspe1591`

## Notice

**warning:** Consolidation Is a Choice, Not a Requirement

ASPE permits a parent to elect consolidation, the cost method, or the equity method for subsidiaries – a fundamental divergence from IFRS 10 and ASBE No. 33, which mandate consolidation.
