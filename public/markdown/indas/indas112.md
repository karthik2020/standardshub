# Disclosure of Interests in Other Entities

Requires disclosures about an entity's interests in subsidiaries, joint arrangements, associates, and unconsolidated structured entities, substantially converged with IFRS 12.

## Notice

**success:** Substantially converged with IAS/IFRS

Ind AS 112 reproduces IFRS 12's disclosure objectives and requirements almost exactly. It operates alongside Schedule III's own group-entity disclosure formats under the Companies Act, 2013.
