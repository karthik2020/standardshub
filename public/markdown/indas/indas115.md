# Revenue from Contracts with Customers

Establishes a five-step model for recognising revenue from contracts with customers, substantially converged with IFRS 15, with disclosure relaxations for unlisted Indian companies.

## Notice

**success:** Substantially converged with IAS/IFRS

Ind AS 115 reproduces IFRS 15's five-step revenue recognition model almost exactly. The genuine Indian nuance lies in certain disclosure relaxations granted to unlisted/smaller Indian companies, not present in IFRS 15 for a comparable population.
