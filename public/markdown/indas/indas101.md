## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Converged with, but Not Identical to, IFRS

Ind AS 101 is India's national standard, issued by the MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) under the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, converged with IFRS 1 but containing SPECIFIC CARVE-OUTS/CARVE-INS reflecting Indian regulatory and legal context. Section 11 below details every material difference from IFRS 1.
