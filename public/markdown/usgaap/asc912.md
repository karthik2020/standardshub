# ASC 912 – Contractors–Federal Government

Addresses allowable versus unallowable costs, CAS disclosure, and claim recognition for federal government contractors.

ASC 912 governs cost accounting for contractors under the FAR and CAS, including the distinction between allowable and unallowable costs, consistency requirements, and recognition of claims and requests for equitable adjustment. It has no dedicated IFRS counterpart due to its US-specific regulatory framework.

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No dedicated IFRS counterpart exists for ASC 912 because it is tightly integrated with US federal procurement regulations (FAR/CAS) that have no parallel in international standards.
