# Social Benefits

Improves the relevance, faithful representation, and comparability of information about social benefits, covering recognition, measurement, presentation, and disclosure of social benefit liabilities and expenses under the general approach and the optional insurance approach. Effective 1 January 2023.

IPSAS 42 Social Benefits improves the relevance, faithful representation, and comparability of information about social benefits. It prescribes the recognition, measurement, presentation, and disclosure of social benefit liabilities and expenses, covering the general approach (recognizing a liability when each beneficiary satisfies eligibility criteria, measured at best estimate of cost of fulfillment) and the optional insurance approach (applying an insurance-contracts standard by analogy). Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2023.
