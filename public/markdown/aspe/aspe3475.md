# Disposal of Long-lived Assets and Discontinued Operations

Disposal of Long-lived Assets and Discontinued Operations is part of the Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises (ASPE).

This standard is part of the ASPE framework issued by the Canadian Accounting Standards Board. It provides guidance on disposal of long-lived assets and discontinued operations.
