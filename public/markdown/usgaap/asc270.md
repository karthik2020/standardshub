# Interim Reporting

Governs interim financial reporting, including the integral view of interim periods, minimum disclosure requirements, and the estimated annual effective tax rate.

ASC 270 governs interim financial reporting for entities that prepare interim financial statements, applying the integral view that treats each interim period as part of the annual period.
