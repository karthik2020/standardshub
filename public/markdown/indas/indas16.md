## Notice

**warning:** Ind AS – Converged with IAS 16, with a Genuine Useful Life Interaction

Ind AS 16's recognition, measurement, and revaluation model mirrors IAS 16 closely. The distinctive Indian feature is Schedule II of the Companies Act, 2013, which PRESCRIBES indicative useful lives for common asset classes – a level of prescriptive guidance IAS 16 deliberately avoids globally. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
