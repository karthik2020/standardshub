# Revenue from Exchange Transactions

Prescribes, for accrual-basis entities, the accounting for revenue from exchange transactions – recognizing revenue when it is probable that future economic benefits or service potential will flow to the entity and those benefits can be measured reliably, measured at the fair value of consideration received or receivable. Covers services, goods, interest, royalties, and dividends; principal vs agent; and related disclosures. Effective 1 July 2002 (paras. 1–42).

IPSAS 9 Revenue from Exchange Transactions prescribes the accounting for revenue from exchange transactions. It requires recognizing revenue when it is probable that future economic benefits or service potential will flow to the entity and those benefits can be measured reliably, measured at the fair value of consideration received or receivable. It covers revenue from the rendering of services, the sale of goods, and the use of assets yielding interest, royalties, and dividends, plus principal vs. agent determination, direct and indirect cash-flow statement presentation, and related disclosures.
