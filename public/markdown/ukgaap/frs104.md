# FRS 104 – Interim Financial Reporting | Extended Reference

FRS 104 sets out the minimum content and principles for interim financial reporting for entities within the FRS 102/103 suite.

FRS 104 applies to entities preparing interim reports under the FRS 102 or FRS 103 framework, requiring a condensed balance sheet, profit and loss account, statement of changes in equity, and statement of cash flows, together with selected explanatory notes. It mandates consistent accounting policies between interim and annual reports, with materiality assessed relative to the interim period's own financial data. As the counterpart of IAS 34, it provides a proportionate framework for half-yearly and other interim reporting without reproducing a full annual report.

## Notice

**info:** FRS 104 vs IAS 34

FRS 104 is the UK counterpart to IAS 34, setting minimum content for interim reports under the FRS 102/103 suite. It shares IAS 34's philosophy of providing an update rather than a miniature annual report, while remaining aligned with the UK GAAP framework.
