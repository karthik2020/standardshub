SIC-3 — Elimination of Unrealised Profits and Losses on Transactions with Associates
Historical reference note · Issued 1997 · Withdrawn 2003
⚠ WITHDRAWN — Superseded by the 2003 revision of IAS 28 Investments in Associates, effective for periods beginning 1 January 2005
What SIC-3 addressed
SIC-3 clarified how an investor should measure its share of UNREALISED profits and losses arising from transactions between itself (or its consolidated subsidiaries) and its ASSOCIATES, when applying the equity method under IAS 28 — specifically, whether and to what extent such unrealised amounts should be eliminated.
The conclusion it reached
Unrealised profits and losses on transactions with associates should be ELIMINATED to the extent of the investor's INTEREST in the associate. However, unrealised LOSSES should NOT be eliminated to the extent they provide evidence of an IMPAIRMENT of the asset transferred — recognising that a loss reflecting genuine impairment is not merely an intercompany accounting artefact.
Where this guidance lives today
Superseded byIAS 28 Investments in Associates and Joint Ventures (revised 2003, and subsequently)
Why withdrawnThe elimination principle was incorporated directly into the main standard's equity method guidance, making a standalone interpretation unnecessary
Current statusThe underlying principle remains good law today — it simply now sits in IAS 28 itself rather than in a separate SIC
