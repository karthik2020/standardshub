# Financial Statement Discussion and Analysis

Provides guidance on preparing and presenting management discussion and analysis of an entity's financial statements, covering operations, objectives, strategies, financial statement analysis, and principal risks and uncertainties.

RPG 2 Financial Statement Discussion and Analysis guides the preparation of narrative analysis from the entity's perspective to complement IPSAS 1 financial statements. It sets out four core content elements: overview of operations and environment, objectives and strategies, analysis of financial statements (including trends and budget reconciliation under IPSAS 24), and principal risks and uncertainties.
