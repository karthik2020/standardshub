# Earnings Per Share

Governs the calculation and presentation of earnings per share (EPS), including basic EPS, diluted EPS, and the treasury stock and if-converted methods.

ASC 260 governs the calculation and presentation of earnings per share (EPS) for entities whose common stock is publicly held or that file with the SEC, including the treasury stock method, if-converted method, antidilution, and the two-class method.
