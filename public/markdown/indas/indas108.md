Prescribes the disclosure of information about operating segments, mirroring IFRS 8's management approach and quantitative thresholds almost exactly, with the main Indian-specific interaction being with Schedule III of the Companies Act, 2013.

## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IFRS 8

Ind AS 108 mirrors IFRS 8's management approach and quantitative thresholds almost exactly. The main Indian-specific interaction is with Schedule III of the Companies Act, 2013 (governing overall financial statement FORMAT), not with segment identification itself. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
