# Accounting Standard for Measurement of Inventories

Accounting Standard for Measurement of Inventories is part of the Japan GAAP framework.

This standard is part of the Japan GAAP framework issued by the Accounting Standards Board of Japan (ASBJ). It provides guidance on accounting standard for measurement of inventories.
