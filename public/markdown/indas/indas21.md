## Notice

**warning:** Ind AS – Converged with IAS 21, with a Notable Historical Transitional Difference

Ind AS 21's functional/presentation currency framework and exchange difference recognition rules mirror IAS 21 closely. India historically permitted an OPTIONAL exemption (carried over from earlier Indian GAAP practice) allowing CAPITALISATION of exchange differences on certain long-term foreign currency monetary items into related asset cost, rather than immediate P&L recognition as IAS 21 requires – a transitional relief that has now LARGELY EXPIRED for most entities. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
