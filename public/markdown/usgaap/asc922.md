# ASC 922 – Entertainment–Cable Television

Addresses capitalization of initial subscriber installation costs and prematurity period costs for cable television systems.

ASC 922 covers capitalizable hookup costs, the prematurity period during which cable operators defer certain costs while developing subscriber bases, and the subsequent expensing of operating costs once systems mature. No dedicated IFRS cable television standard exists.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No dedicated IFRS cable standard exists. ASC 922's specific guidance on hookup costs and the prematurity period has no direct IFRS counterpart.
