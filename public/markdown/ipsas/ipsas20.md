# Related Party Disclosures

Prescribes disclosures about the nature of an entity's related-party relationships, the compensation of its key management personnel, and the amount of transactions with related parties. Effective 1 January 2004.

IPSAS 20 Related Party Disclosures prescribes disclosures of relationships and transactions between an entity and its related parties.
