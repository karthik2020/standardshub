# Financial Reporting in Hyperinflationary Economies

Prescribes accounting and disclosure for entities whose functional currency is that of a hyperinflationary economy – restating the financial statements (SOFP, statement of financial performance, and cash flow statement) into the measuring unit current at the reporting date using a general price index, with an exit rule when the economy ceases to be hyperinflationary. Includes Appendix A on first-year restatement and deferred tax. Effective 1 July 2002 (paras. 1–39).

IPSAS 10 Financial Reporting in Hyperinflationary Economies prescribes the accounting and disclosure for entities whose functional currency is that of a hyperinflationary economy. It requires restating financial statements into the measuring unit current at the reporting date using a general price index, treating monetary items as exempt from restatement, deriving the gain or loss on net monetary position, applying an exit rule when the economy ceases to be hyperinflationary, and applying Appendix A on first-year restatement and deferred tax.
