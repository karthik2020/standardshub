# Stock-based Compensation and Other Stock-based Payments

Stock-based Compensation and Other Stock-based Payments is part of the Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises (ASPE).

This standard is part of the ASPE framework issued by the Canadian Accounting Standards Board. It provides guidance on stock-based compensation and other stock-based payments.
