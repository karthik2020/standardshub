# Information Reflecting the Effects of Changing Prices

The IASC's most fully developed response to general price-level changes, offering guidance on both current cost accounting (CCA) and current purchasing power (CPP) approaches. Withdrawn in 2003 as global inflation stabilised. For current price-level guidance, IAS 29 addresses only hyperinflationary economies.
