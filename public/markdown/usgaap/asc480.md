# Distinguishing Liabilities From Equity

Establishes the criteria for classifying financial instruments as liabilities or equity, including mandatorily redeemable instruments, obligations to repurchase shares, and mezzanine equity.

ASC 480 establishes the classification criteria for distinguishing liabilities from equity for financial instruments, including mandatorily redeemable instruments, obligations to repurchase equity, and puttable shares.
