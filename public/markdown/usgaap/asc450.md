# Contingencies

Governs loss and gain contingencies, including the three probability tiers, range estimation, litigation disclosures, and insurance recoveries.

ASC 450 governs contingencies, including loss contingencies (recognised when probable and reasonably estimable) and gain contingencies (not recognised until realised).
