# FRS 105 – The Financial Reporting Standard Applicable to the Micro-entities Regime | Extended Reference

FRS 105 is the micro-entities regime, providing the lightest-touch tier of UK GAAP for the smallest qualifying companies with turnover not more than £632,000 and balance sheet total not more than £316,000.

FRS 105 simplifies recognition and measurement relative to FRS 102, removing fair value options for investment property and fixed asset investments, and requires only a minimal set of mandatory disclosure notes. Micro-entities may also benefit from further Companies Act filing simplifications, such as filing only a balance sheet without a publicly disclosed profit and loss account. Unlike IAS for SMEs, which offers a single simplified standard, UK GAAP structures simplification across two tiers: FRS 102 Section 1A for small entities and FRS 105 for micro-entities.

## Notice

**info:** FRS 105 vs IFRS for SMEs

FRS 105 provides a further simplification below FRS 102 for the smallest qualifying companies, removing fair value options and elective accounting policies. It reflects the same proportionate philosophy as the IFRS for SMEs but at an even lighter-touch tier appropriate for micro-entities.
