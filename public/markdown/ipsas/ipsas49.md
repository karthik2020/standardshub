# Retirement Benefit Plans

Prescribes financial reporting requirements for public sector retirement benefit plans as separate reporting entities, covering fair value measurement of plan investments, defined benefit obligations using projected salary levels, and mandatory direct-method cash flow statements. Effective 1 January 2026.

IPSAS 49 Retirement Benefit Plans specifies the accounting and reporting principles for public sector retirement benefit plans (defined contribution, defined benefit, and hybrid plans) as reporting entities separate from the employers/sponsors. It mandates fair value measurement for all plan investments, requires the actuarial present value of promised retirement benefits to be calculated using projected salary levels, establishes statement formats (statement of financial position, statement of changes in net assets available for benefits, and a mandatory direct-method cash flow statement), and requires comprehensive disclosures on funding policies, actuarial assumptions, and plan descriptions. Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2026.
