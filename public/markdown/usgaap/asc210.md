# Balance Sheet

Governs the format and classification of the Balance Sheet, including current/non-current classification, order-of-liquidity presentation, and offsetting rules.

ASC 210 governs the presentation of the Balance Sheet, including the choice between classified and unclassified presentations, and the circumstances in which assets and liabilities may be offset.
