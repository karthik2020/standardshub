Conceptual Framework Linkage

Communicating the policies actually applied, clearly and concisely
ASPE Section 1505 REQUIRES an ENTERPRISE to DISCLOSE its SIGNIFICANT accounting POLICIES – the MEASUREMENT bases USED and the SPECIFIC policies NECESSARY for a PROPER understanding of the FINANCIAL statements – PRESENTED in a CLEAR and CONCISE manner, TYPICALLY within the FIRST note to the FINANCIAL statements.
2

Scope – Applying to Every Set of ASPE Financial Statements

A universal disclosure requirement
This Section applies to EVERY complete SET of GENERAL-purpose FINANCIAL statements PREPARED in ACCORDANCE with ASPE, REGARDLESS of the ENTERPRISE'S size or INDUSTRY – accounting POLICY disclosure is a UNIVERSAL, foundational EXPECTATION.
3

What Counts as a "Significant" Accounting Policy

Policies genuinely necessary for proper understanding
A SIGNIFICANT accounting POLICY is ONE that is GENUINELY necessary for a PROPER understanding of the FINANCIAL statements – TYPICALLY, policies INVOLVING a CHOICE among ALTERNATIVES permitted BY GAAP, policies UNIQUE to the ENTERPRISE'S particular INDUSTRY, or policies INVOLVING SIGNIFICANT measurement JUDGMENT.
4

Where Policy Disclosure Appears – the Initial Note

A conventional, expected location
SIGNIFICANT accounting POLICIES are NORMALLY disclosed WITHIN the FIRST note to the FINANCIAL statements – a CONVENTION that HELPS readers LOCATE this INFORMATION EFFICIENTLY, and PROVIDES essential CONTEXT before MOVING into the MORE detailed, TRANSACTION-specific notes THAT follow.
5

Measurement Bases Used

Historical cost, fair value, or another basis, clearly identified
WHERE MORE than ONE measurement BASIS is USED within the FINANCIAL statements (e.g. HISTORICAL cost FOR most items, FAIR value FOR certain financial INSTRUMENTS UNDER Section 3856), the ENTERPRISE clearly IDENTIFIES which BASIS applies to WHICH categories of ASSETS and LIABILITIES.
6

Focusing on Judgment and Choice, Not Boilerplate

Meaningful disclosure, not a mechanical recitation of every possible policy
Section 1505 EXPLICITLY emphasizes that POLICY disclosure should FOCUS on policies GENUINELY unique TO the entity OR industry, or THOSE involving SIGNIFICANT judgment OR a choice AMONG alternatives PERMITTED by GAAP – RATHER than a MECHANICAL, boilerplate RECITATION of EVERY conceivable accounting POLICY an enterprise MIGHT apply. THIS is a GENUINELY practical, focused APPROACH, AVOIDING disclosure OVERLOAD that PROVIDES limited GENUINE value to READERS.
7

Industry-Specific and Entity-Specific Policies

Where an enterprise's particular circumstances warrant extra explanation
POLICIES that are PARTICULAR to a SPECIFIC industry (e.g. LONG-term construction CONTRACTS, agricultural PRODUCTION) or UNIQUE to an ENTERPRISE'S own CIRCUMSTANCES (e.g. an UNUSUAL revenue ARRANGEMENT) WARRANT EXPLICIT disclosure, GIVEN their LIKELY significance TO a reader'S UNDERSTANDING.
8

Worked Example – Disclosing a Genuinely Significant Judgment-Driven Policy

A policy genuinely worth explaining
Example: An income tax accounting policy choice
A private enterprise elects to apply the Taxes Payable Method (rather than the Future Income Taxes Method) for its income tax accounting under Section 3465.
The COMPANY discloses THIS policy CHOICE clearly WITHIN its INITIAL accounting POLICIES note – GIVEN it REPRESENTS a GENUINE choice AMONG two PERMITTED alternatives UNDER ASPE, and MATERIALLY affects HOW readers should INTERPRET the ABSENCE of any DEFERRED tax balances ELSEWHERE in the FINANCIAL statements.
9

Worked Example – Avoiding Boilerplate Disclosure

Skipping a policy disclosure that adds no genuine value
Example: A routine, non-judgmental cash policy
A private enterprise holds ordinary operating bank account balances, with no unusual cash management arrangements, foreign currency holdings, or cash equivalent classification judgments involved.
GIVEN the ROUTINE, non-JUDGMENTAL nature of this BALANCE, the COMPANY need NOT include an EXTENSIVE, boilerplate "CASH accounting POLICY" note – CONSISTENT with Section 1505'S focus ON genuinely SIGNIFICANT policies, RATHER than MECHANICAL completeness.

Quick-Reference Glossary

Significant accounting policy
A policy genuinely necessary for a proper understanding of the financial statements, typically involving a choice among GAAP-permitted alternatives or significant judgment.
Boilerplate disclosure
Generic, mechanically-recited policy language that provides limited genuine value to readers, which Section 1505 discourages in favor of focused, meaningful disclosure.
