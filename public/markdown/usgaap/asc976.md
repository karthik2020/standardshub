# ASC 976 – Real Estate–Retail Land

Governs retail land sales revenue recognition, rescission periods, and inventory costing under the relative sales value method.

ASC 976 covers the historical retail land sales method largely superseded by ASC 606, current treatment of rescission periods and refund obligations, and inventory costing using the relative sales value method. It is broadly converged with IFRS post-IFRS 15 on revenue recognition for land sales.
