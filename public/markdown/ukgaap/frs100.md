# FRS 100 – Application of Financial Reporting Requirements | Extended Reference

FRS 100 is the overarching framework standard that directs UK and Irish entities to the correct component of the new UK GAAP suite. It does not prescribe recognition or measurement requirements itself, but sets out the legal and regulatory hierarchy governing framework choice.

FRS 100 applies to all entities preparing financial statements intended to give a true and fair view that are not required to use EU/UK-adopted IFRS. It establishes the decision tree for selecting between FRS 101, FRS 102, FRS 103, FRS 104, and FRS 105, and includes provisions for the statutory true and fair override. Unlike US GAAP's more prescriptive approach, UK GAAP is explicitly anchored to the IFRS family at every tier.

## Notice

**info:** Relationship to IAS/IFRS

Every tier of new UK GAAP is explicitly anchored to the IFRS family, representing a deliberate alignment strategy. This differs from US GAAP's historically independent development path, even where the two frameworks have subsequently converged on specific topics.
