# Reporting on the Long-Term Sustainability of an Entity's Finances

Guides reporting on the long-term sustainability of public sector finances over specified time horizons across service, revenue, and debt dimensions, incorporating demographic, economic, and sustainability program assumptions.

RPG 1 Reporting on the Long-Term Sustainability of an Entity's Finances provides good-practice guidance for preparing forward-looking projections of future inflows and outflows, evaluating capacity and vulnerability across service, revenue, and debt dimensions, and presenting transparent disclosures on principles, assumptions, discount rates, and sensitivity analysis.
