DRS 9 — Bilanzierung von Anteilen an Gemeinschaftsunternehmen im Konzernabschluss
Accounting for interests in jointly-managed enterprises in consolidated financial statements (proportionate consolidation)
⛔ WITHDRAWN 2018 — replaced by DRS 27
Part of the DRS consolidation wave
DRS 9, first announced 11 December 2001, elaborated the proportionate consolidation mechanics for jointly-managed enterprises established under §310 HGB, covered in detail in the earlier HGB poster series — the genuine election (unlike full consolidation's mandatory 100% approach) allowing a joint venturer to bring in only its own percentage share of each underlying asset and liability line by line.
Why withdrawn — a clean, confirmed handoff
This is one of the most cleanly documented replacements in this consolidation wave: DRS 9 was formally withdrawn upon the BMJV's 17 July 2018 Bundesanzeiger announcement of DRS 27 "Anteilmäßige Konsolidierung," with DRS 9 remaining applicable for the LAST time only to fiscal years beginning on or before 31 December 2019 — a genuine, confirmed one-to-one handoff between predecessor and successor, unlike some of the earlier, more consolidative replacements in this wave.
