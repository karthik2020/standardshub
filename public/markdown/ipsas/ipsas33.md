# First-time Adoption of Accrual Basis IPSAS

Guides a first-time adopter in preparing its statement of financial position on transition to the accrual basis of financial reporting under IPSAS – using deemed-cost as the core transitional tool, three-year transitional relief for assets, liabilities, revenue and transfer expenses, and specific exemptions that do not affect fair presentation. Effective 1 January 2017. (IFRS 1 equivalent, adapted for the public sector.)

IPSAS 33 First-time Adoption of Accrual Basis IPSAS guides a public sector entity that is adopting the accrual basis of financial reporting for the first time. It prescribes the basis for preparing the opening statement of financial position, the use of deemed cost as the core transition tool, three-year transitional relief for assets, liabilities, revenue and transfer expenses, and a set of exemptions – some of which do not affect fair presentation – that ease the transition while requiring full retrospective disclosure.
