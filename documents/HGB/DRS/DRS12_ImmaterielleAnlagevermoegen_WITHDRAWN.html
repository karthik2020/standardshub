DRS 12 — Immaterielle Vermögensgegenstände des Anlagevermögens
Intangible fixed assets
⛔ WITHDRAWN 2016 — replaced by DRS 24
Part of the DRS consolidation wave
DRS 12 addressed the recognition and measurement of intangible assets within the fixed-asset category, supplementing the general HGB framework (§§246, 248, 255, established in the earlier HGB poster series) — a genuinely important area given the recurring interpretive challenges around self-created intangibles, brand values, and similar items arising in practice.
Why withdrawn — folded into the 2016 group-accounts-specific rewrite
DRS 12 was withdrawn effective for fiscal years beginning after 31 December 2016, replaced by DRS 24 "Immaterielle Vermögensgegenstände im Konzernabschluss" — part of the SAME coordinated 23 February 2016 release that also introduced DRS 22 and DRS 23. DRS 24's own explicit focus on the CONSOLIDATION-specific dimension (particularly purchase-price allocation following a business acquisition) reflects a genuine shift in emphasis toward group-accounting particulars, rather than simply restating DRS 12's own more general treatment.
