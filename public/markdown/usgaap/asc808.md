# Collaborative Arrangements

Governs the accounting for collaborative arrangements where two or more parties jointly control an activity and share risks and rewards.

ASC 808 addresses collaborative arrangements, also known as collaborative arrangements or joint operating agreements, where two or more parties act as principals and have joint control. The Topic provides guidance on income statement presentation and the distinction between collaborative arrangements and contracts with customers under ASC 606.
