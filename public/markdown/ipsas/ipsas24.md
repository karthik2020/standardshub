# Presentation of Budget Information in Financial Statements

Prescribes how to present approved budget information alongside the financial statements, including budget-vs-actual reconciliation, multi-year budgets, comparable-basis disclosures, and note disclosures on basis, period and scope. Effective 1 January 2009.

IPSAS 24 Presentation of Budget Information in Financial Statements prescribes how to present approved budget and actual amounts in the financial statements, including reconciliations and comparable-basis disclosures.
