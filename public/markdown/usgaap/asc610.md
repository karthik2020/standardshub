# Other Income

Governs gains and losses from sales of nonfinancial assets to non-customers, including sale-leaseback transactions and partial sales.

ASC 610 governs gains and losses from sales of nonfinancial assets to non-customers, including the recognition of gains on sale-leaseback transactions and partial sales of assets.
