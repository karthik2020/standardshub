# ASC 950 – Financial Services–Title Plant

Addresses title plant cost capitalization, indefinite-life treatment without amortization, and maintenance cost expensing.

ASC 950 covers capitalization criteria for title plant costs, the indefinite-life treatment that eliminates amortization while the title plant remains adequately maintained, and expensing of maintenance costs. It also addresses bulk purchases and startup costs. No dedicated IFRS title plant standard exists.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No dedicated IFRS title plant standard exists. ASC 950's indefinite-life treatment and specific capitalization criteria for title plants have no direct IFRS counterpart.
