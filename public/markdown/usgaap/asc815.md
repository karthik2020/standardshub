# Derivatives and Hedging

Governs the accounting for derivative instruments and hedging activities, including fair value, cash flow, and net investment hedges.

ASC 815 addresses the recognition, measurement, presentation, and disclosure of derivative instruments and hedging activities. It requires all derivatives to be recorded on the balance sheet at fair value, with hedge accounting permitted for qualifying hedging relationships under specific criteria.

## Notice

**warning:** Key IFRS 9 Divergence

IFRS 9 eliminated embedded derivative bifurcation for financial asset hosts; ASC 815 retains bifurcation for both financial and non-financial hosts. Hedge accounting models also differ significantly between ASC 815 and IFRS 9.
