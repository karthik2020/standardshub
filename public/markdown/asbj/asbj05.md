# Accounting Standard for Presentation of Net Assets in the Balance Sheet

Accounting Standard for Presentation of Net Assets in the Balance Sheet is part of the Japan GAAP framework.

This standard is part of the Japan GAAP framework issued by the Accounting Standards Board of Japan (ASBJ). It provides guidance on accounting standard for presentation of net assets in the balance sheet.
