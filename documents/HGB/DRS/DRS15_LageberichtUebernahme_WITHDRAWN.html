DRS 15 / DRS 15a — Lagebericht / Übernahmerechtliche Angaben im Konzernlagebericht
Management report / Takeover-law disclosures within the group management report
⛔ WITHDRAWN — folded into DRS 20
Part of the DRS consolidation wave
DRS 15 was, alongside DRS 5 (risk reporting), the DRSC's original general standard elaborating the Konzernlagebericht content requirements under §315 HGB, established in detail in the earlier HGB poster series. Its companion, DRS 15a, addressed specifically the takeover-law disclosures required for listed parent AGs (paralleling §315a HGB's own takeover-related disclosure content, also documented in that earlier poster) — the "Übernahmerelevante Angaben" this whole HGB series flagged when covering §289a/§315a.
Why withdrawn — the centrepiece of the entire DRS management-report consolidation
DRS 15 and DRS 15a, together with DRS 5 (risk reporting) and its own sector variants, were ALL folded into the single, comprehensive DRS 20 "Konzernlagebericht" — the genuine centrepiece of this entire consolidation wave. Rather than requiring practitioners to cross-reference several separate, narrower standards to understand the full Konzernlagebericht requirements, DRS 20 consolidates business review, forecast, risk, opportunity, internal control, and takeover-related disclosure content into one unified document, adopted 14 September 2012.
