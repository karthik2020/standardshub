## Notice

**warning:** Ind AS – Converged with IAS 12, with One Genuine Indian-Specific Addition

Ind AS 12's balance sheet approach to deferred tax mirrors IAS 12 closely, but Appendix A to Ind AS 12 addresses a UNIQUELY INDIAN concept: MAT (Minimum Alternate Tax) credit entitlement under Section 115JAA of the Income Tax Act, 1961, recognised as a deferred tax asset when specified recognition criteria are met – a concept with NO direct IAS 12 equivalent. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
