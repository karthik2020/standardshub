# Borrowing Costs

Requires borrowing costs directly attributable to the acquisition, construction or production of a qualifying asset to be capitalised as part of the asset's cost, converged almost exactly with IAS 23.

## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IAS 23

Ind AS 23 reproduces IAS 23's mandatory borrowing cost capitalisation model for qualifying assets almost exactly, with no substantive divergence. Its Indian practical relevance is prominent for infrastructure/real estate developers with extended construction periods. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
