# ASC 972 – Real Estate–Common Interest Realty Associations

Addresses common property capitalization, regular and special assessments, and replacement fund accounting for common interest realty associations.

ASC 972 covers the capitalization policy choice for common property, revenue recognition for regular versus special assessments, replacement fund reserve accounting, and worked examples of special assessments. No dedicated IFRS CIRA standard exists.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No dedicated IFRS CIRA standard exists. ASC 972's specific guidance on common property capitalization and assessment accounting for realty associations has no direct IFRS counterpart.
