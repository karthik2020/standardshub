# Interest

Addresses the recognition, measurement, and disclosure of interest costs and interest income.

ASC 835 provides guidance on accounting for interest costs and interest income. It addresses when interest should be capitalised as part of the cost of an asset, how interest income should be measured for debt instruments, and the disclosure requirements related to interest.

**Issued:** 1980-06

**Latest Amendment:** 2020-06

## Notice

**info:** Broadly Converged with IAS 23

ASC 835's interest capitalisation requirements are broadly converged with IAS 23. Both frameworks require interest costs to be capitalised as part of the cost of a qualifying asset, using similar criteria and measurement approaches.
