# ASC 908 – Airlines

Addresses accounting for major maintenance costs and frequent-flyer programs in the airline industry.

ASC 908 covers three acceptable methods for accounting for major overhaul costs, revenue recognition for mileage programs under ASC 606, and route acquisition costs. It provides worked examples contrasting maintenance methods and frequent-flyer program revenue deferral.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No dedicated IFRS industry standard exists for airlines. While general IFRS standards apply, ASC 908's specific industry guidance on mileage programs and major maintenance lacks a direct counterpart.
