# StandardsHub

## Vision

StandardsHub is a modern educational reference website for IAS, IFRS and Ind AS standards.

Its goal is to help students and professionals quickly understand accounting standards through structured explanations, revision posters and intuitive navigation.

The website should feel more like a modern documentation platform than a traditional blog.

---

# Target Audience

Primary

- ACCA students
- CA students
- CMA students
- Accounting professionals

Secondary

- Teachers
- Universities
- Finance professionals

---

# Success Criteria

A visitor should be able to:

- Find a standard within a few seconds.
- Understand its objective and key requirements.
- Revise the standard using the poster.
- Navigate easily between related standards.
- Use the website comfortably on desktop and mobile.

---

# Non-functional Requirements

- Fast loading
- Accessible
- Responsive
- Search engine friendly
- Maintainable codebase
- Reusable components

## Business Model

StandardsHub will follow a freemium model.

Free users
- Full access to educational content.
- Non-intrusive advertisements.

Premium users
- No advertisements.
- Additional productivity features introduced in future versions.

Advertising must never interrupt reading or reduce usability.
