# Cash Flow Statements

Prescribes the presentation of a cash flow statement as an integral part of the financial statements for each period presented, requiring classification by operating, investing, and financing activities, and disclosures for non-cash transactions, changes in financing liabilities, and cash-equivalent components.

IPSAS 2 Cash Flow Statements prescribes how to present cash flow statements prepared under the accrual basis, including classification by operating, investing, and financing activities, the direct and indirect methods, net-basis and foreign-currency cash flows, non-cash transactions, reconciliations of financing liabilities, and related disclosures.
