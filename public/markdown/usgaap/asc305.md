# Cash and Cash Equivalents

Governs the definition, recognition, and disclosure of cash and cash equivalents, including restricted cash and compensating balance arrangements.

ASC 305 governs cash and cash equivalents, including the definition of cash equivalents, restricted cash, compensating balances, and overdrafts.
