# Related Party Disclosures

Requires disclosure of related party relationships and transactions to ensure transparency in financial reporting.

ASC 850 requires entities to disclose related party relationships and transactions in their financial statements. It defines related parties, requires disclosure of material related party transactions, and mandates disclosure of the nature of the relationship and the transaction terms, enabling users to assess whether transactions occurred on arm's-length terms.

## Notice

**warning:** Divergence from IAS 24

ASC 850's related party scope and disclosure requirements differ from IAS 24 in several areas, including the treatment of key management personnel compensation disclosures and the definition of close family members. IAS 24 also requires disclosure of compensation categorised by type, which ASC 850 does not explicitly mandate.
