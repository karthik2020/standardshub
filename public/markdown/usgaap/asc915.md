# ASC 915 – Development Stage Entities (Legacy)

Legacy topic requiring inception-to-date reporting for development stage entities, superseded by ASU 2014-10.

ASC 915 formerly required development stage entities to present inception-to-date cumulative financial statements and additional disclosures. ASU 2014-10 eliminated these special reporting requirements effective for annual periods beginning after December 15, 2014, and such entities now apply normal GAAP supplemented by ASC 205-40 going concern disclosures.

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No direct IFRS equivalent exists for this legacy US GAAP topic. IFRS has no specific development-stage entity framework, and ASC 915 has been superseded by ASU 2014-10.
