# ASC 948 – Financial Services–Mortgage Banking

Governs mortgage loans held for sale, interest rate lock commitments, and mortgage servicing rights for mortgage banking entities.

ASC 948 addresses measurement of mortgage loans held for sale, derivative treatment of interest rate lock commitments, forward loan sale commitments, and mortgage servicing rights with cross-references to ASC 860. No dedicated IFRS mortgage banking standard exists.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## History & Development

History identifier: `asc948`

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No dedicated IFRS mortgage banking standard exists. While general IFRS standards on financial instruments apply, ASC 948's specific mortgage banking guidance lacks a direct counterpart.
