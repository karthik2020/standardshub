## Notice

**success:** Substantially converged with IAS/IFRS

Ind AS 110 reproduces IFRS 10's power/returns/linkage control model almost exactly. The key Indian nuance concerns how the investment entities exception interacts with India's SEBI-regulated mutual fund and AIF structures.
