# Inventories

Prescribes measurement of inventories at the lower of cost and net realizable value (or current replacement cost for no/nominal-charge distribution), cost formulas, NRV write-downs and reversals, and fair-value disclosures. The Stripping Costs amendment (Nov 2024, effective 2027) is embedded authoritatively.

IPSAS 12 Inventories prescribes the measurement and disclosure of inventories, including lower of cost and NRV, current replacement cost for give-away inventory, cost formulas, and NRV write-downs.
