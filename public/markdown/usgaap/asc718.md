# Compensation–Stock Compensation

Governs equity-classified and liability-classified share-based payment arrangements, including fair value measurement and expense recognition.

ASC 718 governs compensation cost arising from share-based payment transactions, including stock options, restricted stock, performance shares, and cash-settled awards, requiring fair value measurement for equity-classified awards and remeasurement for liability-classified awards.
