# Leases

Withdrawn standard. IPSAS 13 Leases was withdrawn for periods beginning 1 January 2025 and superseded by IPSAS 43 Leases, which adopts a single lessee model bringing leases on-balance-sheet and eliminating the finance/operating lessee split.
