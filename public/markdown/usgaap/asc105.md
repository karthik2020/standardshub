# Generally Accepted Accounting Principles

Establishes the FASB Accounting Standards Codification as the single source of authoritative U.S. GAAP for nongovernmental entities, governing the structure, amendment process, and hierarchy of US GAAP.

ASC 105 establishes the FASB Accounting Standards Codification (the Codification) as the single source of authoritative U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) for nongovernmental entities.
