# Construction Contracts

Prescribes the accounting for construction contract costs and revenue – including the percentage-of-completion method, contract cost inclusions/exclusions, the immediate-expected-deficit rule (limited to full-cost-recovery contracts), and the treatment of commercial versus non-commercial public-sector contracts. Effective 1 July 2002.

IPSAS 11 Construction Contracts prescribes the accounting treatment of costs and revenue for construction contracts, adapted for the public sector where funding is often via appropriation or grant rather than contract revenue.
