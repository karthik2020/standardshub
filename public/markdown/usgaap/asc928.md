# ASC 928 – Entertainment–Music

Addresses royalty advances, music license agreements, and the distinction between functional and symbolic intellectual property in the music industry.

ASC 928 covers royalty advances to artists, licensing of music and master recordings distinguishing functional from symbolic IP, and accounting for free goods and promotional distribution. It is broadly converged with IFRS 15 on revenue recognition, though no dedicated IFRS music standard exists.

## Notice

**info:** Converged with IFRS 15

ASC 928's license revenue recognition is broadly converged with IFRS 15, though no dedicated IFRS music standard exists. The functional versus symbolic IP distinction aligns with IFRS 15's performance obligations.
