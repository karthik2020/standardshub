# Exploration for and Evaluation of Mineral Resources

Prescribes the recognition and measurement of exploration and evaluation expenditures for mineral resources, mirroring IFRS 6 almost exactly, with the main Indian-specific context relating to the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act.

## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IFRS 6

Ind AS 106 mirrors IFRS 6 almost exactly, since IFRS 6 itself is a deliberately narrow, policy-choice-preserving standard. The main Indian-specific context relates to interaction with India's Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, not the accounting itself. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
