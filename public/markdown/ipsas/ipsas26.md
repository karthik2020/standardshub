# Impairment of Cash-Generating Assets

Specifies how an entity assesses and measures impairment of cash-generating assets – including identifiable cash-generating units and goodwill. Uses the higher of an asset's fair value less costs to sell and its value in use; covers identifying assets susceptible to impairment, measuring recoverable amount, allocating impairment losses within a CGU, reversing impairment, and related disclosures. Effective 1 April 2009. (IAS 36 equivalent, adapted for the public sector.)

IPSAS 26 Impairment of Cash-Generating Assets specifies how a public sector entity assesses and measures impairment of cash-generating assets – those assets that generate cash flows in the entity's principal revenue-generating activities. It requires identifying assets that may be impaired, measuring recoverable amount as the higher of fair value less costs to sell and value in use (using a detailed, entity-specific cash-flow projection), allocating impairment losses first to assets other than goodwill and only thereafter to goodwill, allocating losses within a cash-generating unit in a prescribed order, reversing impairment when the reasons for an impairment loss have reversed, and disclosing the amount of impairment recognised and the methods used.
