# The Conceptual Framework for General Purpose Financial Reporting by Public Sector Entities

Establishes the foundational concepts, objectives, qualitative characteristics, reporting entity boundaries, elements (assets, liabilities, revenue, expense), recognition criteria, measurement bases, and presentation rules for public sector GPFRs.

The Conceptual Framework for General Purpose Financial Reporting by Public Sector Entities underpins all accrual-basis IPSAS and RPGs. Spanning 8 chapters, it sets out the objectives of financial reporting (accountability and decision-making for service recipients and resource providers), qualitative characteristics (relevance, faithful representation, understandability, timeliness, comparability, verifiability), reporting entity definitions, element definitions (service potential and economic benefits), recognition criteria, measurement bases (historical cost, current operational value, cost of fulfillment, fair value), and presentation principles (display vs disclosure).
