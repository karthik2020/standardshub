Prescribes the accounting treatment for inventories, including cost measurement, NRV and cost formulas, converged almost exactly with IAS 2 with the main Indian nuance being GST input tax credit interaction.

## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IAS 2

Ind AS 2 reproduces IAS 2's cost measurement, NRV, and cost formula requirements almost exactly. The main Indian practical nuance concerns how GST input tax credits interact with inventory cost measurement. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
