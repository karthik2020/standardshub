Ind AS 16 — Property, Plant and Equipment
Extended reference poster · Converged with IAS 16 · Schedule II useful lives — a genuine Indian companion regulation · Worked examples
🇮🇳 IND AS — CONVERGED WITH IAS 16, WITH A GENUINE USEFUL LIFE INTERACTION — Ind AS 16's recognition, measurement, and revaluation model mirrors IAS 16 closely. The distinctive Indian feature is Schedule II of the Companies Act, 2013, which PRESCRIBES indicative useful lives for common asset classes — a level of prescriptive guidance IAS 16 deliberately avoids globally. Conceptual Framework Linkage

Identical recognition/measurement philosophy to IAS 16
Ind AS 16 applies the IDENTICAL principle as IAS 16 — PPE is recognised when it is PROBABLE future economic benefits will flow to the entity and cost can be measured RELIABLY, initially at COST, with a choice of cost or revaluation model subsequently.
HOW IND AS 16 MAPS TO THE FRAMEWORK
Faithful representation — componentised depreciation reflecting different useful lives of significant parts — identical to IAS 16Schedule II useful lives — the Companies Act, 2013's Schedule II PRESCRIBES indicative useful lives for common asset categories (buildings, plant, vehicles, etc.), which companies may REBUT with technical justification — a genuinely Indian companion regulation, distinct from IAS 16's own entirely principles-based, entity-specific useful life estimation
Ind AS 16 introduced NO substantive change to the recognition/measurement model relative to IAS 16 — Schedule II operates as a REGULATORY BENCHMARK for useful lives under company law, not a modification of Ind AS 16's own accounting principles.

Scope & Applicability

What Ind AS 16 covers
Ind AS 16 applies to tangible items held for use in production/supply, rental, or administration, expected to be used for more than one period — IDENTICAL scope to IAS 16.
Key definitions you must know
Componentisation
IDENTICAL requirement to IAS 16 — significant parts with different useful lives are depreciated separately.
Schedule II
A schedule to the Companies Act, 2013 prescribing indicative useful lives for depreciation purposes across common asset classes.

Recognition Criteria

Identical two-pronged test to IAS 16
Probable future economic benefits AND reliable cost measurement — IDENTICAL recognition criteria to IAS 16, applied item by item (or by significant component).

Initial Measurement — Cost

Identical to IAS 16
Purchase price, directly attributable costs to bring the asset to working condition, and initial estimate of decommissioning/restoration costs — IDENTICAL to IAS 16.

Subsequent Measurement — Cost vs Revaluation Model

Identical to IAS 16
Both models are available under Ind AS 16, IDENTICAL to IAS 16, with revaluation surpluses recognised in OCI (revaluation reserve) and increases reversing prior revaluation decreases through P&L — same rules as IAS 16.

Depreciation (with worked example)

Identical systematic allocation principle to IAS 16
Example 1: Useful life estimate vs Schedule II benchmark
An Indian manufacturing company's engineering team assesses that a specialised piece of plant machinery has a USEFUL LIFE of 20 years based on its specific operating conditions and maintenance regime, while Schedule II's INDICATIVE useful life for general plant and machinery is 15 years.
Under Ind AS 16's OWN principles (identical to IAS 16), the entity uses its OWN technically justified estimate of 20 years, provided this is appropriately DOCUMENTED and DISCLOSED as a departure from the Schedule II indicative life — illustrating that Schedule II is a REBUTTABLE benchmark, not a mandatory override of Ind AS 16's own entity-specific useful life principle.

Schedule II Useful Lives — The Indian Companion Regulation

A genuinely important practical Indian consideration
Schedule II to the Companies Act, 2013 provides INDICATIVE useful lives for depreciation purposes (e.g. general plant and machinery: 15 years; office buildings: 60 years; motor vehicles: 8-10 years), serving as a REGULATORY BENCHMARK. While Ind AS 16 (like IAS 16) requires useful life to be an ENTITY-SPECIFIC technical/commercial estimate, in PRACTICE many Indian companies align closely with Schedule II's indicative lives absent strong grounds to depart, given the compliance comfort this provides under company law — a distinctly Indian INTERACTION between statutory guidance and Ind AS 16's own principles-based estimate.

Derecognition

Identical to IAS 16
An asset is derecognised on disposal or when no future economic benefits are expected, with gain/loss (disposal proceeds less carrying amount) recognised in P&L — IDENTICAL to IAS 16.
