# ASC 995 – U.S. Steamship Entities

Addresses Capital Construction Fund deferred tax treatment for U.S. steamship entities under the Merchant Marine Act.

ASC 995 covers the Capital Construction Fund created under Merchant Marine Act Section 607 and the associated deferred tax exception for CCF deposits. It is a genuinely US-specific maritime tax construct with no direct IFRS equivalent.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No direct IFRS equivalent exists. ASC 995 addresses a uniquely US maritime tax construct under the Merchant Marine Act that has no parallel in IFRS.
