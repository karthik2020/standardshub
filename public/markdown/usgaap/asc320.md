# Investments–Debt and Equity Securities

Governs investments in debt and equity securities, including held-to-maturity, trading, and available-for-sale classifications; now primarily governs debt securities after ASU 2016-01.

ASC 320 governs investments in debt and equity securities, including classification, measurement, and disclosure requirements for trading, available-for-sale, and held-to-maturity securities.
