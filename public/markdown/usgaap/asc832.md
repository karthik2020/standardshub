# Government Assistance

Requires business entities to disclose information about certain types of government assistance received, without prescribing recognition or measurement guidance.

ASC 832 requires business entities to disclose the nature, financial statement effects, and significant terms of government assistance transactions, but does not prescribe how such assistance should be recognized or measured.

**Issued:** 2021-05

**Latest Amendment:** 2021-05

**Effective:** 2021-12-15

## Notice

**warning:** Disclosure Standard Only

ASC 832 is a disclosure standard only. It does NOT prescribe how a business entity should recognize or measure government assistance in its financial statements – that determination is left entirely to existing GAAP (often applied by analogy, since no specific US GAAP recognition model exists for government assistance to business entities). This stands in sharp contrast to IAS 20 / ASBE 16, which are full recognition-and-measurement standards.
