## Notice

**warning:** Important Indian modifications – limited applicability

Ind AS 114 is an optional standard of limited practical relevance in India. Indian regulated entities generally follow CERC/SERC tariff regulations separately, and previous Indian GAAP did not customarily recognise regulatory deferral account balances as assets/liabilities in the way IFRS 14 contemplates.
