# Leases

Requires lessees to recognise right-of-use assets and lease liabilities for virtually all leases, while retaining a dual-model for lessors.

ASC 842 superseded ASC 840 and fundamentally changed US GAAP lease accounting. Lessees must recognise right-of-use assets and corresponding lease liabilities for all leases with terms greater than 12 months. Lessors retain a three-model classification (sales-type, direct financing, and operating leases), which differs from IFRS 16's single lessor model.

## Notice

**warning:** Key IFRS 16 Divergence

ASC 842 retains a dual lessor model (sales-type/direct financing vs operating leases) while IFRS 16 eliminated the operating lease classification for lessors entirely. Both frameworks require lessee balance-sheet recognition, but the lessor accounting models remain structurally different.
