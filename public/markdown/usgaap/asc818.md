# Environmental Credits and Environmental Credit Obligations

Establishes the first dedicated US GAAP model for recognizing, measuring, presenting, and disclosing environmental credits and environmental credit obligations.

ASC 818 establishes a dedicated accounting model for environmental credits and environmental credit obligations (ECOs), driven by intended use and combining cost-based and fair-value measurement within a single liability.

**Issued:** 2026-05

**Latest Amendment:** 2026-05

**Effective:** 2027-12-15

## Notice

**info:** Effective Date

Annual periods beginning after December 15, 2027 (including interim periods within) for public business entities; one year later for all other entities. Early adoption is permitted as of the beginning of an annual reporting period. Before ASC 818, US GAAP had no dedicated guidance for environmental credits, leading to significant diversity in practice (inventory, intangible assets, or contingencies models were all used).
