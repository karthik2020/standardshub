# Accounting Standard for Equity Method of Accounting for Investments

Accounting Standard for Equity Method of Accounting for Investments is part of the Japan GAAP framework.

This standard is part of the Japan GAAP framework issued by the Accounting Standards Board of Japan (ASBJ). It provides guidance on accounting standard for equity method of accounting for investments.
