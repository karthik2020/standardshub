# The Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates

Prescribes how to include foreign currency transactions and foreign operations in an entity's financial statements, and how to translate financial statements into a presentation currency. Covers determining functional currency, recognizing exchange differences, translating foreign operations, and disposal effects, including the advance-consideration Appendix.

IPSAS 4 The Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates prescribes how to include foreign currency transactions and foreign operations in an entity's financial statements, and how to translate financial statements into a presentation currency. Principal issues are (a) which exchange rate(s) to use and (b) how to report the effects of changes in foreign exchange rates, including the Appendix on advance consideration.
