# Inventory

Governs inventory measurement, including lower of cost and net realisable value (LCNRV) for FIFO and weighted-average, and lower of cost or market (LCM) for LIFO and retail methods.

ASC 330 governs inventory measurement, including cost formulas (FIFO, weighted-average, LIFO, retail method) and the lower of cost or net realisable value write-down test.
