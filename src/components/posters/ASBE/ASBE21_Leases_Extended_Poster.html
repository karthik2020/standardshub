Conceptual Framework Linkage

Bringing substantially all leases onto the lessee's Balance Sheet
ASBE No. 21 was REVISED to introduce a SINGLE on-BALANCE-SHEET LESSEE model — REQUIRING recognition of a "RIGHT-OF-USE ASSET" and a CORRESPONDING lease LIABILITY for SUBSTANTIALLY all leases — CLOSELY mirroring IFRS 16'S own approach internationally, and ELIMINATING the OLDER dual finance/OPERATING lessee CLASSIFICATION model.
2

Scope and Effective Date

All lessees, with dual classification retained for lessors
This Standard applies to ALL companies that are LESSEES (WITH continued dual CLASSIFICATION for lessor ACCOUNTING — Section 7), effective from JANUARY 1, 2021 for ALL enterprises — a REVISION broadly PARALLELING IFRS 16'S own international ROLLOUT, WITH only a MODEST delay relative to IFRS 16'S 2019 effective date.
3

The Single On-Balance-Sheet Lessee Model

No more off-balance-sheet operating leases
UNDER the REVISED Standard, a LESSEE recognises a RIGHT-OF-USE asset and a LEASE liability for VIRTUALLY all leases — ELIMINATING the OLD "operating LEASE" category'S off-balance-SHEET treatment entirely, SUBJECT to the NARROW exemptions in Section 6 — a SINGLE, unified LESSEE accounting MODEL replacing the PREVIOUS dual finance/OPERATING classification.
4

Initial Measurement

Present value of lease payments, plus adjustments
The LEASE liability is INITIALLY measured at the PRESENT value of REMAINING lease PAYMENTS, discounted USING the rate IMPLICIT in the LEASE (or, WHERE not readily DETERMINABLE, the LESSEE'S incremental BORROWING rate). The RIGHT-OF-USE asset is INITIALLY measured at COST — GENERALLY the initial LEASE liability, PLUS any LEASE payments MADE at or BEFORE commencement, INITIAL direct COSTS, and ESTIMATED restoration COSTS.
5

Subsequent Measurement

Depreciation of the asset, interest accretion on the liability
The RIGHT-OF-USE asset is DEPRECIATED (GENERALLY straight-LINE) over the SHORTER of the LEASE term or the ASSET'S useful LIFE. The LEASE liability is REDUCED as PAYMENTS are MADE, with INTEREST expense ACCRETING the liability OVER time.
6

Exemptions — Short-Term and Low-Value Leases

Narrow relief, mirroring IFRS 16's own exceptions
A LESSEE MAY elect NOT to APPLY the on-BALANCE-SHEET model to SHORT-TERM leases (GENERALLY 12 MONTHS or LESS, with NO purchase OPTION) or LEASES of LOW-VALUE underlying ASSETS — recognising LEASE payments as an EXPENSE on a STRAIGHT-LINE basis INSTEAD, CONSISTENT with IFRS 16's OWN narrow exemptions.
7

Lessor Accounting — Dual Classification Retained

Finance vs operating classification continues for lessors
UNLIKE the LESSEE-side reform, LESSOR accounting CONTINUES to DISTINGUISH between FINANCE leases (SUBSTANTIALLY transferring the RISKS and REWARDS of OWNERSHIP, derecognising the UNDERLYING asset and recognising a LEASE receivable) and OPERATING leases (RETAINING the underlying ASSET on the LESSOR'S own Balance SHEET, recognising LEASE income on a STRAIGHT-LINE basis) — CONSISTENT with IFRS 16'S own RETENTION of dual LESSOR classification internationally.
8

Worked Example — Initial Recognition of a Lease

Recognising the right-of-use asset and lease liability
Example: A 5-year warehouse lease
A company enters a 5-year warehouse lease with annual payments of RMB 15,000,000, discounted using an incremental borrowing rate of 4%, giving a present value of lease payments of approximately RMB 66,800,000.
The company recognises a RIGHT-OF-USE ASSET and a LEASE LIABILITY of approximately RMB 66,800,000 EACH at commencement — REPLACING what WOULD previously have been a SIMPLE straight-LINE rental EXPENSE under the OLD operating lease TREATMENT.
9

Worked Example — the Short-Term Lease Exemption

Retaining off-balance-sheet treatment for a genuinely short lease
Example: A 9-month equipment rental
A company rents equipment for a 9-month project, with no purchase option or expectation of renewal, at RMB 1,500,000 per month.
The company ELECTS the SHORT-TERM lease EXEMPTION, recognising RMB 1,500,000 of RENTAL expense EACH month on a STRAIGHT-line basis — WITHOUT recognising a RIGHT-of-use ASSET or LEASE liability.

Quick-Reference Glossary

Right-of-use asset
The asset recognised by a lessee representing its right to use a leased asset over the lease term.
Incremental borrowing rate
The rate a lessee would have to pay to borrow funds to obtain an asset of similar value, used when the rate implicit in the lease is not readily determinable.
