# Statement of Cash Flows

Governs the Statement of Cash Flows, including classification of cash flows, direct vs indirect method, and restricted cash reporting.

ASC 230 requires a Statement of Cash Flows as part of a complete set of financial statements, governing the three categories of cash flow and the presentation of operating, investing, and financing activities.
