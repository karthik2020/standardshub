# Investment Property

Prescribes the recognition and measurement of investment property, converged with IAS 40 but with a major measurement divergence: the cost model is mandatory and fair value cannot be used for balance sheet measurement.

## Notice

**warning:** Ind AS – Converged with IAS 40, with a Major Measurement Divergence

IAS 40 permits a CHOICE between the cost model and the FAIR VALUE model for subsequent measurement of investment property. Ind AS 40 REMOVES this choice entirely – the COST MODEL is MANDATORY, with fair value change gains/losses NEVER flowing through P&L. Fair value must still be DISCLOSED in the notes, but cannot be used for balance sheet measurement. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
