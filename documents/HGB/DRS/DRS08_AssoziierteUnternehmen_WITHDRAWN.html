DRS 8 — Bilanzierung von Anteilen an assoziierten Unternehmen im Konzernabschluss
Accounting for interests in associated companies in consolidated financial statements (equity method)
⛔ WITHDRAWN — replaced by DRS 26
Part of the DRS consolidation wave
DRS 8 elaborated the equity-method accounting for associated companies established under §§311–312 HGB, covered in detail in the earlier HGB poster series — including the initial measurement choice (book value vs. capital share method), the annual share-of-profit adjustment, and treatment of dividends received, all originally documented at the statutory level in that earlier HGB poster.
Why withdrawn — modernised alongside its joint-venture counterpart
DRS 8 was withdrawn and replaced by DRS 26 "Assoziierte Unternehmen," part of the same broader modernisation wave that also replaced DRS 9's own joint-venture standard with DRS 27 — both amended together via DRÄS 11 (2021), reflecting the DRSC's practice of treating the equity-method and proportionate-consolidation standards as a closely related pair given their shared conceptual roots in HGB §§310–312.
