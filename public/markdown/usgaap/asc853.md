# Service Concession Arrangements

Addresses accounting for service concession arrangements where an operator provides public services on behalf of a grantor.

ASC 853 provides guidance for service concession arrangements – contracts where a private operator builds, upgrades, operates, or maintains public infrastructure on behalf of a government grantor. The operator does not recognise the infrastructure as PPE; instead, it recognises an intangible asset or receivable depending on the payment structure.

## Notice

**info:** Converged with IFRIC 12

ASC 853 was added specifically to converge with IFRIC 12 (Service Concession Arrangements). Both frameworks use the same two-model approach (intangible asset vs receivable) and the same control indicators for scope, ensuring consistent accounting for PPP arrangements globally.
