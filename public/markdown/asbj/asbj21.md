# Accounting Standard for Business Combinations

Accounting Standard for Business Combinations is part of the Japan GAAP framework.

This standard is part of the Japan GAAP framework issued by the Accounting Standards Board of Japan (ASBJ). It provides guidance on accounting standard for business combinations.
