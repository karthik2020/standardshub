# Statement of Shareholder Equity

Governs the presentation of the Statement of Shareholders' Equity, reconciling opening and closing balances of each equity component.

ASC 215 governs how equity movements are presented in the Statement of Shareholders' Equity, reconciling each equity caption from opening to closing balance.
