# Income Taxes

Income Taxes is part of the Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises (ASPE).

This standard is part of the ASPE framework issued by the Canadian Accounting Standards Board. It provides guidance on income taxes.

**Issued:** 2006-02

**Latest Amendment:** 2016-01

**Effective:** 2006-02-01

## History & Development

History identifier: `aspe3465`

## Notice

**warning:** A Genuine Choice Between Two Tax Models

Section 3465 allows an enterprise to elect the Taxes Payable Method with no deferred tax assets or liabilities – an accounting policy choice with no equivalent under IAS 12, ASC 740, or ASBE No. 18.
