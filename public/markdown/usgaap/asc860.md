# Transfers and Servicing

Addresses the accounting for transfers of financial assets, including the derecognition criteria and servicing arrangements.

ASC 860 governs the accounting for transfers of financial assets, establishing criteria for sale accounting versus secured borrowing treatment. It requires isolated assets, transferee rights to pledge/exchange, and absence of effective control by the transferor for derecognition. The standard also addresses servicing assets and liabilities, and repurchase agreements.

## Notice

**warning:** Key IFRS 9 Divergence

ASC 860 uses a control-based test as the sole gatekeeping analysis for derecognition, whereas IFRS 9 primarily uses a risks-and-rewards test, falling back to control only in specific circumstances. This structural divergence means identical transfers can produce different balance-sheet outcomes under US GAAP and IFRS.
