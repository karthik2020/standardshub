# Impairment of Non-Cash-Generating Assets

Prescribes the accounting for impairment of long-lived assets that are NOT cash-generating, using recoverable service amount (the primary measurement), fair value less costs to sell, and reversal of impairment when the cause has reversed. Effective 1 January 2006.

IPSAS 21 Impairment of Non-Cash-Generating Assets prescribes how to assess and measure impairment of assets that are not cash-generating, including recoverable service amount, fair value less costs to sell, and reversal of impairment.
