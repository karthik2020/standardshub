DRS 4 — Unternehmenserwerbe im Konzernabschluss
Business combinations in consolidated financial statements
⛔ WITHDRAWN 2016 — replaced by DRS 23
Part of the DRS consolidation wave
DRS 4, first issued in August 2000, was the DRSC's original standard elaborating capital consolidation and business combination accounting for group accounts — directly supplementing HGB §301's own capital consolidation mechanics, covered in detail in the earlier HGB poster series. It was last amended for BilMoG (2009) changes before its final withdrawal.
Why withdrawn — a fundamental rewrite, not a minor update
Rather than incrementally amend DRS 4 again, the HGB-Fachausschuss undertook a GROUND-UP rewrite, addressing numerous practical issues DRS 4 had never resolved — including the mechanics of multi-tier ("mehrstufiger") group structures, given HGB's own conceptual model assumes a single-tier group as its baseline. The result, DRS 23 "Kapitalkonsolidierung (Einbeziehung von Tochterunternehmen in den Konzernabschluss)," was adopted 25 September 2015 and formally announced in the Bundesanzeiger on 23 February 2016, with DRS 4 withdrawn effective for fiscal years beginning after 31 December 2016.
