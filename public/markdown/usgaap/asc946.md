# ASC 946 – Financial Services–Investment Companies

Requires investment companies to measure all investments at fair value and eliminates consolidation of majority-owned investees.

ASC 946 establishes that investment companies must measure all investments at fair value with changes recognized in earnings and permits the NAV practical expedient. It is broadly converged with IFRS 10's amendments for investment entities, allowing measurement of controlled investees at fair value rather than consolidation.

## Notice

**info:** Converged with IFRS 10

ASC 946 and IFRS 10's investment entity amendments are broadly converged. Both frameworks measure investments at fair value and exempt investment entities from consolidating controlled investees.
