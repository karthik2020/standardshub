# Business Combinations

Governs the accounting for business combinations using the acquisition method, including goodwill measurement and bargain purchase gains.

ASC 805 establishes principles and requirements for how an acquirer recognises and measures in its financial statements the identifiable assets acquired, the liabilities assumed, and any noncontrolling interest in the acquiree. It also addresses the recognition and measurement of goodwill or a gain from a bargain purchase.

## Notice

**info:** Converged with IFRS 3

ASC 805 and IFRS 3 are substantially converged. Remaining differences include noncontrolling interest measurement choices and the subsequent measurement of contingent consideration.
