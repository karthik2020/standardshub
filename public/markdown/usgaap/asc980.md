# ASC 980 – Regulated Operations

Governs regulatory asset and liability recognition for rate-regulated entities including discontinuation of regulatory accounting.

ASC 980 establishes a three-part recognition test for regulatory assets and addresses regulatory liabilities, as well as discontinuation of regulatory asset recognition. It keyly diverges from IFRS 14, which provides a narrower interim model rather than a universal regulatory accounting framework.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## Notice

**warning:** Divergence from IFRS 14

ASC 980 provides a comprehensive regulatory accounting model, while IFRS 14 is a narrow interim standard only. The universality of ASC 980's approach contrasts sharply with IFRS 14's limited temporary relief.
