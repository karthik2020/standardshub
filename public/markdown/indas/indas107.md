# Financial Instruments: Disclosures

Prescribes the disclosure requirements for financial instruments, substantively identical to IFRS 7, with the most significant Indian-specific context being interaction with RBI's prudential disclosure requirements for banks and NBFCs.

## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IFRS 7

Ind AS 107 is substantively identical to IFRS 7, with the most significant Indian-specific context being its interaction with RBI's own prudential disclosure requirements for banks and NBFCs. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
