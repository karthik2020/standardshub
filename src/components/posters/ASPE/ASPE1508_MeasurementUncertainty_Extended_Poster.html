Conceptual Framework Linkage

Flagging estimates genuinely at risk of near-term material change
ASPE Section 1508 REQUIRES disclosure OF measurement UNCERTAINTY that EXISTS in the PREPARATION of FINANCIAL statements – SPECIFICALLY, WHERE it IS reasonably POSSIBLE that an ESTIMATE will CHANGE within the NEAR term, and the EFFECT of that CHANGE would BE material. THIS Section GIVES the TOPIC its OWN dedicated, STANDALONE treatment – a GENUINELY distinctive STRUCTURAL choice RELATIVE to IFRS, WHICH addresses SIMILAR ground WITHIN IAS 1'S broader PRESENTATION requirements RATHER than as a SEPARATE standard (Section 8).
2

Scope – Estimates Made at the Balance Sheet Date

Any recognized amount involving significant estimation
This Section applies to ANY recognized AMOUNT within the FINANCIAL statements THAT required a SIGNIFICANT estimate AS of the BALANCE sheet date – COVERING the VALUATION of CERTAIN assets and LIABILITIES that DEPEND on matters GENUINELY uncertain AT that date.
3

The Two-Condition Disclosure Test

Both conditions must be met together
ConditionDescription
1It IS reasonably POSSIBLE that the ESTIMATE could CHANGE by a MATERIAL amount WITHIN the NEAR term
2The EFFECT of that CHANGE, IF it OCCURS, would BE material to the FINANCIAL statements
4

Condition One – Reasonably Possible Change Within the Near Term

A meaningful likelihood, not a remote possibility
"REASONABLY possible" SITS between REMOTE and PROBABLE – a GENUINE, MEANINGFUL likelihood that NEW information OR events COULD cause the ESTIMATE to CHANGE, RATHER than a MERELY theoretical possibility.
5

Condition Two – A Material Effect

The change itself must matter to users
EVEN WHERE an ESTIMATE is GENUINELY uncertain, DISCLOSURE is REQUIRED only IF the POTENTIAL change WOULD be MATERIAL – a HIGHLY uncertain but IMMATERIAL estimate DOES NOT trigger THIS Section'S disclosure REQUIREMENT.
6

What "Near Term" Generally Means

Typically interpreted as within one year
"NEAR term" is GENERALLY interpreted as WITHIN one YEAR of the BALANCE sheet date – FOCUSING disclosure ON estimates GENUINELY at RISK of near-TERM revision, RATHER than SPECULATIVE, longer-HORIZON uncertainty that IS inherent in VIRTUALLY every long-TERM estimate an ENTERPRISE makes.

Common Sources of Measurement Uncertainty

Recurring categories across private enterprise financial statements
Net realizable value of inventoryUseful lives of long-lived assets, underlying depreciation/amortization estimatesContingent liability provisions, including litigation and warranty provisionsAllowance for doubtful accountsImpairment assessments for goodwill and long-lived assets
8

A Dedicated Standard – Unlike IAS 1's Embedded Treatment

A structural choice giving the topic its own standalone visibility
INTERNATIONALLY, IAS 1 addresses "SOURCES of ESTIMATION uncertainty" WITHIN its OWN broader PRESENTATION requirements, RATHER than AS a SEPARATE standard. ASPE, BY contrast, gives THIS topic ITS OWN dedicated SECTION – a STRUCTURAL choice that ELEVATES measurement UNCERTAINTY disclosure to a STANDALONE, explicitly-NAMED requirement, RATHER than ONE requirement AMONG many WITHIN a BROADER presentation STANDARD.
9

Worked Example – Disclosing Uncertainty in a Warranty Provision

Both conditions genuinely met
Example: A warranty provision with genuine near-term revision risk
A private manufacturer recognizes a $900,000 warranty provision, based on historical claims data. A newly identified product defect, still under investigation, could reasonably increase this estimate by $400,000 within the next several months – a material amount relative to the company's overall financial statements.
BOTH conditions ARE met (a REASONABLY possible NEAR-term change; a MATERIAL potential EFFECT), so the COMPANY discloses the NATURE of the UNCERTAINTY and, WHERE reasonably DETERMINABLE, the POTENTIAL $400,000 EFFECT on the FINANCIAL statements.
10

Worked Example – an Estimate That Does Not Meet the Disclosure Threshold

Uncertainty exists, but the disclosure threshold is not met
Example: A routine, well-established estimate
The same company also estimates a $50,000 allowance for doubtful accounts, based on a stable, well-established historical collection pattern with no indication of near-term change.
DESPITE involving AN estimate, THIS balance does NOT meet THIS Section'S disclosure THRESHOLD – THERE is NO reasonably POSSIBLE near-TERM material CHANGE indicated, SO no SPECIFIC measurement uncertainty DISCLOSURE is REQUIRED for this ITEM.

Quick-Reference Glossary

Measurement uncertainty
Uncertainty in a recognized financial statement amount, arising from the estimation involved in its measurement.
Near term
The period, generally interpreted as within one year of the balance sheet date, within which an estimate could reasonably change.
Financial statement key (ASPE terminology)
BS Balance Sheet IS Income Statement SRE Statement of Retained Earnings / Statement of Changes in Equity SCF Cash Flow Statement
