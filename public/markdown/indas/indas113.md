## Notice

**success:** Substantially converged with IAS/IFRS

Ind AS 113 reproduces IFRS 13's single fair value framework and three-level hierarchy almost exactly. Its most notable Indian interaction is with the Registered Valuers framework under the Companies Act, 2013/IBBI.
