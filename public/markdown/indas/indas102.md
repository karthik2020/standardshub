## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Converged with, but Not Identical to, IFRS

Ind AS 102 is India's national standard, near-identical to IFRS 2 in substance, but interacting with SPECIFIC Indian regulatory bodies (SEBI for listed companies' ESOP schemes) and Indian tax treatment of perquisites. Section 11 details the comparison.
