# Financial Instruments: Disclosures

Requires an entity to disclose the significance of its financial instruments and the exposures to which it is subject. Covers classes of financial instruments and significance for financial position, fair-value designations, significance for financial performance, accounting policies, concessionary loans, and the nature and extent of credit, liquidity and market risk (qualitative and quantitative). Effective 1 January 2013. (IFRS 7 equivalent.) Complements IPSAS 28 (presentation) and IPSAS 41 (recognition/measurement).

IPSAS 30 Financial Instruments: Disclosures requires entities to disclose the significance of their financial instruments and the exposures to which they are exposed to risk. It covers the classes of financial instruments and their significance for financial position, fair-value designations, significance for financial performance, accounting policies, concessionary loans, and the nature and extent of credit, liquidity and market risk – both qualitative and quantitative. It complements IPSAS 28 (presentation) and IPSAS 41 (recognition and measurement).
