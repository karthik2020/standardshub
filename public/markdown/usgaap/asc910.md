# ASC 910 – Contractors–Construction

Governs revenue and cost recognition for long-term construction contracts, including over-time methods and contract modifications.

ASC 910 establishes the percentage-of-completion method for recognizing revenue over time on construction contracts and addresses capitalizable contract costs, combining and segmenting contracts, and loss provisions. It is broadly converged with IFRS 15 for long-term contract revenue recognition.

## Notice

**info:** Converged with IFRS 15

ASC 910's over-time revenue recognition through input or output methods is broadly converged with IFRS 15's requirements for long-term contracts, providing consistent global guidance for construction revenue.
