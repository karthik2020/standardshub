# Foreign Currency Matters

Addresses foreign currency transactions and the translation of foreign entity financial statements.

ASC 830 governs the accounting for foreign currency transactions, including the recognition of foreign currency gains and losses, and the translation of financial statements of foreign operations into the reporting currency. It establishes the functional currency concept and the current rate method for translating foreign entity financial statements.

**Issued:** 1981-12

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1981-12-01

## Notice

**warning:** Divergence from IAS 21

ASC 830's functional currency determination and foreign currency translation model differs from IAS 21 in several areas, including the treatment of certain foreign operations and the translation of financial statements of hyperinflationary foreign entities.
