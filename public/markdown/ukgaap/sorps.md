# SORPs – Statements of Recommended Practice | Extended Reference

Statements of Recommended Practice (SORPs) provide sector-specific guidance on applying FRS 102 within particular industries, such as charities, further and higher education, registered social housing, LLPs, and pension schemes.

SORPs are developed by recognised SORP-making bodies under FRC oversight and supplement the general FRS 102 framework with practical, industry-specific presentation and disclosure guidance. While not themselves binding Financial Reporting Standards, departure from a relevant SORP must be justified and disclosed, giving them genuine comply-or-explain practical force. The most prominent SORP is the Charities SORP, which requires the Statement of Financial Activities in place of a conventional profit and loss account, analogous in purpose to US GAAP's ASC 958.

## Notice

**info:** UK-Specific Practice Statements

SORPs are sector-specific application guides that supplement FRS 102 within particular industries, providing practical guidance not available in the general standards. They have no direct IAS/IFRS equivalent, as IFRS itself contains no dedicated not-for-profit standard, making SORPs a distinctive feature of the UK GAAP landscape.
