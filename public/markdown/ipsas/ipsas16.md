# Investment Property

Prescribes accounting for investment property – recognising, measuring (current value model by default, with an historical-cost option forproperties that cannot be measured reliably), transferring the asset, and the linked-liability exception. Effective 1 January 2008.

IPSAS 16 Investment Property prescribes the accounting treatment for investment property, including the current value (fair value) model, the historical cost option, and transfers into or out of investment property.
