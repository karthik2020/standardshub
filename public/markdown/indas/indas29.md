# Financial Reporting in Hyperinflationary Economies

Prescribes the price-index-based restatement of financial statements in hyperinflationary economies, converged with IAS 29, of narrow but genuine Indian relevance where an Indian parent has a foreign operation in a hyperinflationary country.

## Notice

**warning:** Ind AS – Converged with IAS 29, of Narrow but Genuine Indian Relevance

Ind AS 29's price-index-based restatement model mirrors IAS 29 closely, with no substantive divergence. India itself has NEVER been classified as hyperinflationary, so Ind AS 29 is IRRELEVANT to Indian domestic operations – but it applies whenever an Indian parent has a FOREIGN OPERATION (subsidiary, branch) based in a country classified as hyperinflationary (e.g. historically Argentina, Venezuela, Zimbabwe, Lebanon, Sudan, Turkey at various points). Section 11 covers the full comparison.
