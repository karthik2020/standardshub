## Notice

**warning:** Ind AS – Converged with IAS 34, with a Genuine Regulatory-Format Interaction

Ind AS 34's recognition/measurement principles for interim periods mirror IAS 34 closely. IAS 34 itself does NOT mandate interim reporting – this is left to jurisdictional regulation. In India, SEBI's LODR (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations MANDATE quarterly financial results for listed companies, using a SPECIFIC prescribed format (Regulation 33) that layers additional presentation requirements on top of Ind AS 34's own principles. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
