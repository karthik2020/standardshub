# Nonmonetary Transactions

Addresses the accounting for nonmonetary transactions, including exchanges of assets and nonreciprocal transfers.

ASC 845 provides guidance on the measurement and recognition of nonmonetary transactions, such as exchanges of property, plant, and equipment, and nonreciprocal transfers like dividends in kind. It requires such transactions to be measured at fair value unless the exchange lacks commercial substance, in which case the carryover basis applies.

## Notice

**info:** Broadly Converged with IAS 16

ASC 845's commercial substance test and fair value measurement approach are broadly converged with IAS 16's guidance on exchanges of assets. Both frameworks generally require fair value measurement, with a carryover-basis exception when an exchange lacks commercial substance.
