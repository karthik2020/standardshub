## Notice

**success:** Ind AS – Closely Converged with IAS 41

Ind AS 41 reproduces IAS 41's fair value less costs to sell model for biological assets and agricultural produce almost exactly, with no substantive divergence (bearer plants are carved out to Ind AS 16, identical to the IAS 41/IAS 16 amendment). Its Indian practical relevance is prominent for plantation, dairy, and poultry companies. Section 11 covers the full comparison.
