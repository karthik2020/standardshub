# ASC 952 – Franchisors

Governs initial franchise fees, continuing royalty fees, and area/master franchise arrangements under ASC 606.

ASC 952 addresses initial franchise fee recognition and allocation under ASC 606, the private company practical expedient from ASU 2021-02, continuing royalty fees under the sales-based royalty exception, and area/master franchise arrangements. It is broadly converged with IFRS 15 on franchise revenue recognition.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**info:** Converged with IFRS 15
