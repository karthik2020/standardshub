# Events after the Reporting Date

Prescribes when entities adjust for events after the reporting date, what to disclose about the authorization-for-issue date, and going-concern assessment – with post-reporting-date events that arose AFTER the reporting date disclosed, not adjusted. Effective 1 January 2008 (revised edition that superseded the 2001 original).

IPSAS 14 Events after the Reporting Date prescribes the accounting and disclosure for events occurring between the reporting date and the date the financial statements are authorized for issue.
