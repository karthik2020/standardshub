Conceptual Framework Linkage

Identical disclosure philosophy to IFRS 12
Ind AS 112 applies the IDENTICAL objective as IFRS 12 – enabling users to EVALUATE the nature of, and risks associated with, an entity's interests in subsidiaries, joint arrangements, associates, and unconsolidated structured entities, and the EFFECTS of those interests on financial position/performance.
HOW IND AS 112 MAPS TO THE FRAMEWORK
Relevance – group structure risks/dependencies are not always apparent from consolidated totals alone – identical to IFRS 12Interaction with Schedule III – the Companies Act, 2013's Schedule III ALSO requires specific group entity disclosures (e.g. a statement showing net assets/profit-loss share of each entity in a consolidation) – operating ALONGSIDE, not instead of, Ind AS 112's own requirements
This dual disclosure requirement (Ind AS 112 + Schedule III) is a genuinely distinctive INDIAN practical feature, though NOT a substantive divergence in Ind AS 112's own accounting content.

Scope & Applicability

What Ind AS 112 covers
Ind AS 112 applies to entities with interests in subsidiaries, joint arrangements, associates, or unconsolidated structured entities – IDENTICAL scope to IFRS 12.
Key definitions you must know
Structured entity
IDENTICAL definition to IFRS 12 – an entity designed so voting rights are not the dominant factor in control.
Schedule III statement
The Companies Act, 2013-mandated statement disclosing each group entity's share of net assets and profit/loss within a consolidation.
3

Significant Judgements in Determining Control/Influence

Identical to IFRS 12
Disclosure of significant judgements/assumptions in determining control, joint control, or significant influence – IDENTICAL to IFRS 12, particularly relevant for de facto control scenarios common in Indian dispersed shareholding structures (see Ind AS 110, Section 4).
4

Disclosures for Subsidiaries

Identical to IFRS 12
Composition of the group, NCI's proportion of ownership/voting rights, summarised financial information for MATERIAL NCI, and nature/extent of significant restrictions on group asset access – IDENTICAL to IFRS 12.
5

Disclosures for Joint Arrangements & Associates (with worked example)

Identical to IFRS 12
Example 1: Summarised financial information for a material Indian joint venture
An Indian listed company holds a 40% interest in a material joint venture SPV constructing a toll road. The JV's total assets are ₹200 crore, total liabilities ₹120 crore, revenue ₹35 crore, profit ₹8 crore.
Ind AS 112 requires disclosure of the JV's summarised financial information (assets, liabilities, revenue, profit) IN FULL, reconciled to the carrying amount of the equity-accounted investment – an IDENTICAL disclosure requirement to IFRS 12 for an equivalent material joint venture globally.
6

Unconsolidated Structured Entities

Identical to IFRS 12
Nature/purpose, size, financing, and MAXIMUM EXPOSURE TO LOSS disclosures for unconsolidated structured entities – IDENTICAL to IFRS 12, relevant for Indian NBFCs' securitisation SPVs where control is NOT established.
7

Schedule III Group Disclosure Interaction

A parallel Companies Act requirement
Schedule III mandates a SPECIFIC additional statement in consolidated financial statements, showing each entity's (parent, subsidiaries, associates, joint ventures) SHARE of consolidated net assets and profit/loss – a REQUIREMENT distinct from, but complementary to, Ind AS 112's own detailed narrative and quantitative disclosures. This is a purely PRESENTATIONAL Companies Act requirement layered on top of Ind AS 112, not a substantive divergence from IFRS 12.
8

Restrictions and Risks

Identical to IFRS 12
Disclosure of significant restrictions (e.g. regulatory, contractual) on a subsidiary's ability to transfer funds to the parent – IDENTICAL to IFRS 12, relevant for Indian subsidiaries subject to RBI/FEMA (Foreign Exchange Management Act) restrictions on cross-border fund transfers.
