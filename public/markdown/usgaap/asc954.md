# ASC 954 – Health Care Entities

Governs patient service revenue, charity care, and functional expense reporting for health care entities.

ASC 954 addresses patient service revenue recognition net of contractual allowances and implicit price concessions, the exclusion of charity care from revenue, the shift from bad debt expense to implicit price concessions, and continuing care retirement community accounting. No dedicated IFRS health care standard exists.

**Issued:** 1974-07

**Latest Amendment:** 2017-01

**Effective:** 1974-07-01

## History & Development

History identifier: `asc954`

## Notice

**warning:** No Direct IFRS Equivalent

No dedicated IFRS health care standard exists. ASC 954's specific guidance on patient service revenue, charity care, and CCRCs has no direct IFRS counterpart.
