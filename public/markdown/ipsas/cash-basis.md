# Financial Reporting Under the Cash Basis of Accounting

Prescribes general purpose financial reporting requirements under the cash basis of accounting, featuring mandatory Part 1 requirements (cash receipts/payments statement, notes, budget comparison) and voluntary Part 2 encouraged disclosures.

Cash Basis IPSAS (revised 2017) provides a comprehensive standard for public sector entities reporting on the cash basis. Part 1 establishes mandatory requirements for recognizing cash receipts, payments, and balances controlled by the entity, foreign currency translation, and comparison of budget and actual amounts where budgets are published. Part 2 identifies encouraged good practices (administered transactions, consolidated financial statements, third-party payments, external assistance, and hyperinflation) to prepare entities for eventual transition to accrual IPSAS via IPSAS 33.
