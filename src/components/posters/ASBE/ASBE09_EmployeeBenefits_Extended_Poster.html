Conceptual Framework Linkage

All forms of consideration for employee service
ASBE No. 9 governs ALL forms of CONSIDERATION given by an ENTERPRISE in EXCHANGE for SERVICES rendered by EMPLOYEES, or for the TERMINATION of employment – BROADLY paralleling IAS 19's OWN four-category STRUCTURE internationally (short-term BENEFITS, post-employment BENEFITS, termination BENEFITS, other LONG-term benefits), though the PRACTICAL emphasis DIFFERS significantly GIVEN China's PREDOMINANTLY state-run PENSION system (Section 4).
2

Mirroring IAS 19's structural categories

Termination benefitsPayments made on termination of employment before the normal retirement date
Other long-term employee benefitsLong-service awards and similar benefits not falling into the other three categories
3

Short-Term Employee Benefits

Recognised as an expense and liability as service is rendered
SHORT-TERM employee BENEFITS – wages, BONUSES, and the ENTERPRISE'S own STATUTORY social INSURANCE contributions (MEDICAL, unemployment, WORK injury, MATERNITY insurance, PLUS the housing PROVIDENT fund) – are RECOGNISED as a LIABILITY and an EXPENSE in the PERIOD the EMPLOYEE renders the RELATED service, UNDISCOUNTED, given their SHORT-term settlement HORIZON.
4

Post-Employment Benefits – the State Basic Pension System

Contributions to a state-managed pool, expensed as incurred
The OVERWHELMING majority of Chinese ENTERPRISES participate in the STATE'S mandatory BASIC PENSION INSURANCE system – a STATE-managed, POOLED arrangement into WHICH the ENTERPRISE makes REGULAR contributions, WITHOUT retaining ANY further LEGAL or CONSTRUCTIVE obligation BEYOND those CONTRIBUTIONS. This is TREATED, in SUBSTANCE, as a DEFINED CONTRIBUTION plan – CONTRIBUTIONS are SIMPLY EXPENSED as INCURRED, WITH no ACTUARIAL valuation REQUIRED, CONSISTENT with IAS 19's OWN defined contribution TREATMENT internationally.
5

Defined Benefit Plans – a Genuinely Narrow Category in China

A structural feature of ASBE 9 that is rarely triggered in practice
THIS Standard DOES contain a FRAMEWORK for DEFINED BENEFIT plans (REQUIRING actuarial VALUATION, discounting, and the SAME general MECHANICS found under IAS 19 internationally) – BUT, given the DOMINANCE of the STATE'S basic PENSION system DESCRIBED above, GENUINE corporate DEFINED BENEFIT pension OBLIGATIONS are a GENUINELY NARROW, comparatively RARE feature of Chinese enterprise ACCOUNTING, in CONTRAST to jurisdictions (SUCH as JAPAN or many WESTERN economies) WHERE legacy CORPORATE defined BENEFIT plans remain WIDESPREAD and MATERIAL.
6

Termination Benefits

Recognised when the entity can no longer withdraw the offer
TERMINATION benefits are RECOGNISED as a LIABILITY and EXPENSE at the EARLIER of: WHEN the ENTERPRISE can NO LONGER WITHDRAW its OFFER of THOSE benefits, or WHEN the ENTERPRISE recognises RELATED restructuring COSTS – CONSISTENT with IAS 19's OWN termination benefit RECOGNITION trigger internationally.
7

Other Long-Term Employee Benefits

A residual category for benefits not settled within twelve months
OTHER long-TERM employee BENEFITS – such as LONG-SERVICE awards NOT expected to be WHOLLY settled WITHIN twelve MONTHS of the RELATED service being RENDERED – are MEASURED on a BASIS broadly SIMILAR to defined BENEFIT post-employment BENEFITS, though WITH a SIMPLIFIED recognition APPROACH for actuarial GAINS and LOSSES.
8

Worked Example – Recognising Short-Term Compensation

A straightforward period expense
Example: Monthly wages and bonus accrual
A company's employees earn RMB 8,000,000 in wages for the month, plus an anticipated year-end bonus accrual of RMB 1,200,000 for the same month's service.
The company recognises a TOTAL short-term employee benefit EXPENSE and LIABILITY of RMB 8,000,000 + RMB 1,200,000 = RMB 9,200,000 for the MONTH, RECOGNISED as the RELATED service is RENDERED, REGARDLESS of WHEN the AMOUNTS are ACTUALLY paid.
9

Worked Example – State Basic Pension Contributions

A defined contribution expense, with no further obligation
Example: Monthly statutory pension insurance contributions
A company is required to contribute 16% of its total monthly payroll of RMB 10,000,000 to the state's basic pension insurance pool.
The company EXPENSES RMB 10,000,000 × 16% = RMB 1,600,000 for the MONTH as INCURRED – WITH NO FURTHER obligation or ACTUARIAL valuation REQUIRED, since the STATE pension SYSTEM is treated as a DEFINED CONTRIBUTION arrangement from the ENTERPRISE'S perspective.

Quick-Reference Glossary

Basic pension insurance (基本养老保险)
China's mandatory, state-managed pension system, into which enterprises make contributions treated as a defined contribution arrangement.
Housing provident fund (住房公积金)
A mandatory employer/employee-funded housing savings scheme in China, treated as a short-term employee benefit.
